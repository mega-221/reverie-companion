# Leadership

**Inspire comrades, push them to their limits.**  
*Recommended for officers, activists and all who aim to coordinate their allies.*  

## Abilities

#### < Inspire — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on any allied roll and costs 1 ACP. Make an Intuition check with a CL equal to that roll and add +2 to the target roll if you succeed.

#### >> Stand Order — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 allies. If they use a melee attack during their next turn, they gain +3 EVA for 1 turn.

#### > Amplify — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Double the effects of your channeled abilities from instruments until your next turn.

#### >> /> Combat March — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Instrument*
**1/1 SN**  
**Effect:** Allies within your M gain +1 ACC.

#### >> Volley Order — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 allies. If they don’t use an attack during their next turn, they gain +3 ACC and +2 DMG on their next ranged attack and it costs 1 SN less to perform.

#### >> Strike Order — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 allies. During their next turn, their melee attacks may cost 2 less SN to perform and gain +2 ACC. If they do, any enemies flanking them deal +DMG equal to the ACC bonus from flanking to them until the end of their next turn.

#### >> />> Focused Melody — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1 · Weapons: Instrument*
**2/1 SN**  
**Effect:** Allies within your M gain +3 on all non-combat rolls. Also costs 1 destiny point to upkeep.

#### >> /> Echoing Elegy — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1 · Weapons: Instrument*
**2/1 SN**  
**Effect:** Enemies within your M suffer 2 SN per round.

#### >> Fallback Order — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 allies. If they move away from their closest enemy during their next turn they gain +1 Tile of movement during that movement action and gain +4 EVA until the end of their next turn.

#### >> Conservation Order — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 allies. If they don’t use any abilities with a SN cost during their next turn they heal 4 SN up to half of their SL at the end of that turn.

#### >> />> Alluring Aria — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2 · Weapons: Instrument*
**1/1 SN**  
**Effect:** Inflict Pull 1 to enemies within your M and Stun 1 to enemies within your C per round.

#### >> />> Radiant Resonance — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2 · Weapons: Instrument*
**2/2 SN**  
**Effect:** Inflict Push 1 to enemies within your M and Burn 2 to enemies within your C per round.

#### >> Pinpoint Order — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 allies and an enemy. If all allies target that enemy with abilities during this round, the last ally to do this gains a temporary destiny point that lasts until the end of the round and gains +4 ACC and +2 DMG on the ability if it was an attack.

#### >> Aegis Order — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 allies and another creature. Each ally who interacts with the chosen creature using an ability or ends their turn in their A gives them +3 EVA for 1 turn.

#### >>> />> Violent Verse — *Channeled Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 3 · Weapons: Instrument*
**5/2 SN**  
**Effect:** Enemies suffer DMG while inside of specific ranges from you: A-C: 5 DMG M: 3 DMG L: 1 DMG

#### >>> />> Hymn of Hope — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3 · Weapons: Instrument*
**5/2 SN**  
**Effect:** Allies heal 5 SN when this ability is first used or 2 SN on subsequent turns while it remains channeled if they are inside of your M.

#### | Gamble — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Your Leadership orders this turn provide double bonuses, but those who follow them suffer -4 EVA while under their effects unless they spend a destiny point.

#### > Tactical Mastery — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**8 SN**  
**Effect:** You may perform a second Leadership order this turn at 0 ACP cost. It must target the same allies and they must achieve the conditions for both orders to get their benefits.

#### | Cacophony — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4 · Weapons: Instrument*
**10 SN**  
**Effect:** Activate all of your active channeled abilities that use an instrument at once for a single turn. Stop channeling all abilities at the end of your turn.

#### > /> Ode to The Observer — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4 · Weapons: Instrument*
**4/6 SN**  
**Effect:** You and allies within your M gain 1 temporary destiny point that lasts until your next turn.
