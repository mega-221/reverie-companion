# Vessel

**Archetype:** Aberration · Parasite · BloodManipulation  
**Recommended disciplines:** Beast  

> Her appetite wasn’t the same ever since the incident in the forest. In the span of a single day she had developed insomnia, her skin had begun to shed in places, and she couldn’t stand the smell of the blooming lilies. That first night she had been turning in her bed for hours, unable to even close her eyes. The pulsating tumor in her back writhed in pain, worming around her spine. It slithered up and down, massaging the fragile linchpin of her locomotive capabilities. After hours of adjustments, it would finally carve out its spot alongside it, making itself at home. It even promised to let her rest once she fed it. The lowlives had finished their drinks and it was time for them to find some entertainment. The stranger resting on a nearby lamp was chosen as the night’s victim and they moved to surround him. He spoke not a word as one of them punched him in the face, sending a stream of blood down his nose. The stranger could only smile as the attacker fell down to his knees, screaming, clenching his blood-covered and newly melted hand. For her next act she would demonstrate her mastery of hemomancy. A fattened pig was brought onto the stage and cut with a single slash of a scalpel. This was all she would need to work her magic. She steadied her breathing and her pupils dilated as she focused, opening the tiny cut into a gaping wound before finally bursting the intestines of the poor creature, leaving only a pale corpse surrounded by a dark red pool. Between the audience’s cheers and her wicked grin, you could barely tell who was having more fun. Vessels are hosts to vampiric parasites who are granted immense power over blood. In order to remain alive and avoid being consumed by the parasitic worm, they must regularly take toxins to pacify it. While some vessels cannot resist the temptation of power and allow the parasite to grow in order to further it, others will instead double down on the pacification of the creature and force it into submission, allowing them more precise control over their powers.

## Class Features
#### Vampiric Parasite — *Passive*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 0*
**Effect:** The Vessel exists in a constant state of struggle against its patron and intruder, suffering a permanent Conviction as long as they have this condition: Hunger: I will feast on the blood of the recently deceased. This Conviction must be fulfilled within a week of suffering a Crisis or it will trigger another Crisis. Fulfilling it grants the Vessel 1 Destiny Point and increases the rank of Hunger. Ingesting herbal poisons lowers the rank of Hunger by 1 rank but inflicts a -20% maximum VG and SN penalty, rounded up, for two weeks. If Hunger would increase beyond the maximum rank, the Vessel is fully overtaken by the parasite and it fully consumes their body. A surgery may be performed once Hunger is at rank 0 to remove the parasite (CL 22 Medicine). The Vessel heals VG equal to their class rank on all hemomancy attacks that inflict Bleeding.

#### >> Crimson Rain — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 1*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · -2 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack may target up to 3 targets who are already bleeding. Inflict Bleeding 2.

#### >> Sate Hunger — *Utility*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 2*
**0 SN**  
**Effect:** Must target a corpse, a willing or an unaware creature and may only be used once per target. Restore 5 VG and 5 SN and deal 10 DMG to the target if it’s a living creature.

## Path: Veincaller
*Path that fully embraces the parasite, increasing the potency of hemomancy.*

#### Unquenchable Thirst — *Passive*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 2*
**Effect:** Hunger extends to overtake another of your Conviction slots and is treated as having 8 ranks. Ingesting herbal poisons now inflicts a -50% maximum VG and SN penalty, rounded up, for one week. While at less than half of your maximum VG, your hemomancy attacks deal +1 DMG and inflict Bleeding 1.

#### > Violent Urge — *Utility*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Lose half of your maximum VG, rounded up. Your next hemomancy attack inflicts Bleeding 2.

#### >>> Bleed Dry — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 4*
**6 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** If this attack kills the target, gain TVG equal to their maximum VG. Your total of VG and TVGgained this way may not exceed your maximum VG.

## Path: Symbiote
*Path that pacifies the parasite, allowing for less destructive uses of hemomancy at the expense of offensive power. Path Passive Ability:*

#### Parasite Pacification — *Passive*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 2*
**Effect:** You may choose not to inflict DMG with your hemomancy attacks and cleanse Bleeding equal to its level that would usually be inflicted by the attack. Your hemomancy attacks deal -1 DMG and the Bleeding they inflict to enemies is reduced by 1. You no longer suffer VG and SN penalties from drinking herbal poisons.

#### >> Vascular Pulse — *Defensive*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 3*
**3 SN** · +1 EVA  
**Effect:** Inflict Stun 2 to any number of targets inside M or closer. Targets affected by Bleeding suffer additional Stun equal to its level.

#### < Rapid Coagulation — *Utility*
*Requires: Vessel Rank 4*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on you or ally within M being afflicted by Bleeding. Reduce the level of that effect by 2.
