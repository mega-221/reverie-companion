# Injuries

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 20-21 -->

Mentioned previously as a consequence of reaching 0 VG, injuries are exactly what
they sound like. They inflict a constant penalty to specific rolls which would use the
affected body part, and they take a long time to heal. To determine what kind of
injury a character will get upon being knocked out, we consider multiple factors.
Keep in mind that these should be looked at after the encounter has ended.

Injury Severity
Severity is determined by the VG the character has at the end of the encounter. For
every 2 points of VG below 1, the severity increases by 1. For example, a character
who collapsed at 0 or -1 VG will only sustain an injury with the severity of 1,
whereas a character who collapsed at -4 VG will sustain an injury with the severity
of 3.
Injury Penalties
An injury is to be inflicted to a part of the body that is determined by the Narrator
and should preferably align with the method used to inflict it. The penalties of an
injury are always the same:
You gain -X on all rolls that would utilize the injured part of your body where X is the
severity of the injury.
Healing Injuries
Healing happens passively while the character is not performing any demanding
task over long periods of time. By default, it takes 4 weeks to reduce the severity of
an injury by 1.
First Aid
First aid is performed upon combat end and is crucial in making sure the recovery
goes smoothly. First aid can be given by any character by performing a survival or
Medicine skill check with the CL of [ 12 + Severity ]. If successful, the injury will
take significantly shorter to fully heal. If survival was used, each point of severity
will take only 3 weeks to heal. If medicine was used, each point of severity will take
only 2 weeks to heal.
