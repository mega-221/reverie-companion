# Echo

<!-- chapter: 06-disciplines | pages: 160-163 -->  
**Exploit the past, animate constructs.**  
*Recommended for explorers of memory shards, roboticists and ambedo experts.*  

## Abilities

#### >>> Past Insight — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Delve into an available static memory and re-experience it.

#### > Mind Glimpse — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Take a short peek at the target’s current thoughts. The target becomes Analyzed.

#### > Construct Command — *Utility Command*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**0 SN**  
**Effect:** Give a construct you are paired with a Movement action or 2 AP.

#### >> Undivided Attention — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Give a construct you are paired with a Movement action or 2 ACP.

#### >>> Archive Review — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain +2 on your next INT roll outside of combat. This effect persists for 10 minutes and doesn’t stack.

#### >> Frequency Shift — *Utility Action*
*Requires: Class Rank 1 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain +2 on all rolls during your next memory delve. Lasts for 10 minutes and does not stack.

#### | Evasion Protocol — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Give a construct you are paired with +2 EVA until your next turn.

#### | Harm Protocol — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Give a construct you are paired with +2 DMG until your next turn.

#### >>> Memory Intrusion — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Forcefully analyze a target’s memory that you are aware of (Opposed Eidoloscope vs Willpower save).

#### >> Reality Tether — *Utility Action*
*Requires: Class Rank 2 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Roll all saving throws during your next memory delve with advantage. Lasts for 10 minutes.

#### > Destruction Command — *Utility Command*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Give a construct you are paired with +2 ACP, +1 DMG dealt and +1 to all status effects inflicted until your next turn. Make a CL 14 Engineering check. On a failure, the construct suffers a Glitch.

#### > Relocation Command — *Utility Command*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Give a construct you are paired with 2 movement actions. Make a CL 14 Engineering check. On a failure, the construct suffers a Glitch.

#### >>> Silent Intrusion — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**8 SN**  
**Effect:** Secretly analyze a target’s memory that you are aware of (Opposed Eidoloscope vs Perception save). The ability fails and the target becomes aware of the attempt upon failure.

#### >> Assistive Link — *Utility Action*
*Requires: Class Rank 3 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Use your eidoloscope to mentally tether to a willing creature. Both of you may use the skill scores of the other for rolling any non-combat roll at the cost of 5 SN. Lasts for 10 minutes.

#### | Mutual Destruction — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Until your next turn, whenever a construct you are paired with inflicts DMG to an enemy it may deplete up to that much of its armour points and inflict that much break to the enemy. It may also deplete its VG in order to add that much DMG to the next attack that turn. Make a CL 16 Engineering check. On a failure, the construct suffers a Glitch.

#### | / | Purify Signal — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**1/2 SN**  
**Effect:** Give a construct you are paired with +2 ACC. It can not inflict additional DMG from ACC overflow while this ability is being channeled. Upon casting, make a CL 16 Engineering check. On a failure, the construct suffers a Glitch.

#### >>> Memory Insertion — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**12 SN**  
**Effect:** Secretly alter a target’s memory that you have delved into or implant an entirely manufactured one (Opposed Eidoloscope vs Perception save). If the ability fails the target becomes aware of the attempt.

#### >>> Ambedic Imprint — *Utility Action*
*Requires: Class Rank 4 · Weapons: Eidoloscope*
**10 SN**  
**Effect:** Record and imprint the memory you are currently inside of onto an empty ambedic shard.

#### | Consciousness Exertion — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Until your next turn, you may perform abilities that grant AP to your linked constructs at no AP cost but at 3 Strain cost per AP granted. Make a CL 18 Engineering check. On a failure, the construct suffers a Glitch.

#### | Perceptive Anomaly — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Fuse your mind with your linked construct completely, redirecting effects that would affect it to yourself. Until your next turn, all DMG and status effects that construct would suffer are redirected to you instead. You gain a temporary destiny point for each ability you redirect to yourself this way.
