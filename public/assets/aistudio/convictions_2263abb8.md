# Convictions

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 35-36 -->

Convictions are one of the integral systems of Reverie and act as a mechanical
representation of roleplay. They are ideals and rules your characters align
themselves to and follow, and sticking to them yields powerful boons while defying
them strips the characters of destiny’s favour. While being “virtues” characters
adhere to, they also act as metaphysical forces of the world and through this can
provide boons to those who follow them.
There are a total of 16 convictions, 4 representing each of Dunyor’s seasons:
Vamor - pioneers and architects
● Design - I will put the greater design above the needs of any one person.
● Ambition - I will become more than I am, whatever the cost.
● Passion - I will make any sacrifice necessary for that which I love.
● Cunning - I will use any opportunity that benefits me, without hesitation or
guilt.
Bruma - folk heroes and martyrs
● Empathy - I will treat each person with dignity, no matter their station.
● Altruism - I will sacrifice for those who need help without expecting anything
in return.
● Safety - I will ensure the well-being of myself and those close to me above all
else.
● Diligence - I will never compromise my goals by taking shortcuts.
Trumst - rulers and peacekeepers
● Unity - I will do everything in my power to prevent needless conflict.
● Truth - I will speak with integrity, holding others accountable to the same
honesty.
● Order - I will purge degeneracy and fragility.
● Curiosity - I will always pursue new knowledge.
Halice - idealists and custodians
● Beauty - I will safeguard the wonders of the world, both crafted and born of
nature.
● Freedom - I will always pursue liberty for both myself and those around me.
● Survival - I will prioritize my own success, discarding anything that
jeopardizes it.
● Progress - I will always strive for innovation and improvement of the world.

Think of convictions as oaths your characters make to themselves. Each character
starts with at least a single conviction at rank 1 and can acquire up to 3 different
convictions at the same time. For characters starting at higher levels more
Convictions at higher ranks may be appropriate. Convictions can increase in rank in
a similar way to skills - their ranks range from 1-4 (and exceptionally 5) and
increasing them requires a destiny point investment equal to the new rank.
If a character would contradict any of their convictions in a non-insignificant way
they will suffer a crisis.
