# Bard

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 90-93 -->  
**Archetype:** Guide · Auras · Channelling  
**Recommended disciplines:** Leadership  
**Recommended special skills:** Whisper, Lore, Carry, Deception  

> He stood upon the stage, the festival crowd below him, humming along to his melody. They did not know it, but this night would stay with them forever. After a brief pause, he inserted an ambedic crystal into his modified violin, a shard containing the happiest of memories he had shared with his late wife. Though he played a familiar love song, his reproduction gave it a surreal texture as the shard emitted its contents to those who bore witness. The song, a well-known common vessel, had been applied magnificently to instill the most human of memories within the audience. She was the first person they would turn to whenever they would encounter even a slight obstacle. She did not simply reproduce legendary music, she warped it beyond recognition into the specific, unique form for every situation that required it. Her songs turned cowards and lowlives into determined, unstoppable machines with naught but sound and emotion. Her melodies pushed individual heroes to their absolute limits. Her delusional, assuring lyrics lured them to their deaths at the hands of an enemy no sane person would have confronted. Their loss was no matter, though, as she could always record their own final moments and twist them to inspire her next band. He was beyond conviction and beyond care. He was left all alone, and his supply of blood was rapidly running out. He prepared his instrument for the final performance, connecting directly to its ambedic mechanism. The almost silent melody grew in strength as they peppered him with arrows. At first the sound was just grating, a minor inconvenience. When he started deliriously singing, blood bubbling in his mouth, his audience was hit by countless invisible slashes, reflecting his wounds upon them. When he finally collapsed, the song continued to escalate into an incomprehensible cacophony, turning his murderers into a writhing funeral pyre. Bards are artists and amplifiers of emotion. They utilize instruments modified to host ambedic crystals, turning their performances from simple music to powerful psychological auras. Bards inspire their allies beyond anything commonly possible, and they stunt their enemies in ways they can hardly comprehend. This shaping and warping of known songs taps into the powers of the seasons without conviction, relying instead on pure emotion and memory to fuel the process.

## Class Features
#### Transmitter — *Passive*
*Requires: Bard Rank 0*
**Effect:** A specialized ambedic device connected to your instrument allows you to play it even while it is not equipped. Channeled abilities using your instruments only cost you up to 1 AP total to upkeep per turn.

#### | Theme Variation — *Utility*
*Requires: Bard Rank 1*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** If you are already performing a channeled ability with your instrument, immediately perform another one and continue to channel both of them without paying an AP cost. You may only channel up to two abilities at once using this ability.

#### >> Trance — *Defensive*
*Requires: Bard Rank 2*
**0 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Heal 2 Strain for each channeled instrument ability you are currently performing.

## Path: Laureate
*A supportive path that bolsters and rallies its allies to perform unimaginable feats.*

#### Inspiring Reproduction — *Passive*
*Requires: Bard Rank 2*
**Effect:** While channeling 2 or more instrument abilities, allies within their range all gain your choice of the following on each of your turns: ● +2 EVA ● Heal 2 Strain ● Gain 2 THP (unstackable)

#### >> Appoint Champion — *Utility*
*Requires: Bard Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triple the effects of your channeled abilities from instruments that affect a target ally until your next turn (does not stack with other multiplicative effects). That ally also gains 1 temporary destiny point that lasts until your next turn.

#### < Derail Retelling — *Utility*
*Requires: Bard Rank 4*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on you or an ally being hit by an attack by 4 or more points above EVA. The character that triggered this ability gains +8 EVA until your next turn and gains 1 temporary destiny point until the end of your next turn.

## Path: Discordant
*Offensive and disruptive path that applies consistent harm to groups of enemies.*

#### Revolting Reproduction — *Passive*
*Requires: Bard Rank 2*
**Effect:** While channeling 2 or more instrument abilities, enemies within their range all suffer your choice of the following on each of your turns: ● Bleeding 1 (unstackable) ● Burn 1 (unstackable) ● -1 EVA

#### >> Visceral Performance — *Defensive*
*Requires: Bard Rank 3*
**5 SN** · -4 EVA  
**Effect:** Triple the effects of your channeled abilities from instruments that affect enemies until your next turn (does not stack with other multiplicative effects).

#### >> Farewell Theme — *Ranged attack*
*Requires: Bard Rank 4*
**10 SN** · +2 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Use a weapon to reduce yourself to 0 HP and become incapacitated. This attack targets and inflicts DMG on all enemies within your channeled abilities’ ranges equal to their combined Strain cost. Your channeled abilities remain channeled for 2 more turns after this ability is performed, and their effects double on each of those turns. You may suffer a failing instead of an injury for performing this attack.
