# Attributes

<!-- chapter: 02-playing-the-game | pages: 14-14 -->

Attributes are the main bodily properties of each creature that serve as limitations
of their skills. There are 4 different attributes:
● Grit: Your physical body. The resilience and power of flesh and bone.
● Reflex: Your uncontrolled impulses. The precise senses and natural instincts.
● Intellect: Your pointed mind. The library of knowledge and its application.
● Psyche: Your own soul. The internal will and the poetry to communicate it.
These attributes will be abbreviated to GRT, REF, INT and PSY. These colors will
also be used to represent the attributes in related concepts like skills.
Attributes each have 4 available ranks, each rank being achieved by investing a
single attribute point into it and raising the maximum rank skills under it can be
upgraded to by 2. The starting rank of each attribute is 1.
Attributes are important as they serve as a requirement for skill advancement as
well as necessary prerequisites for handling certain items and equipment. They are
much harder to improve than your skills, so be deliberate with investing into them.
