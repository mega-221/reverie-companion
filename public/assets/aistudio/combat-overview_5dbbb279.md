CH III. Survival and Combat
Dangerous situations like combat have additional systems in order to be precisely
handled. Combat itself can come in many forms, be it a short duel, a skirmish, a
vehicular engagement or even an entire army battle or siege. A large portion of the
rulebook and the character sheet is dedicated to these situations and gameplay
loops and they are an integral part of the game.
