# Zeal

<!-- chapter: 06-disciplines | pages: 164-168 -->  
**Profess your convictions, evoke your Season.**  
*Recommended for zealous paragons, powerful sorcerers and all those looking to enact their Season upon the world.*  

## Abilities

#### >> Vital Attunement — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**0 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice You add a target creature to your network of Attunement and heal SN equal to the number of creatures you are attuned to if your SN is higher than or equal to half of your SL, rounded down. You remain attuned to the creature for up to 1 hour.

#### >> Warp Flesh — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**0 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Inflict Bleeding 1. If targeting an armored target, all other effects and DMG of the attack are negated and it inflicts Break 1 instead. Requires Attunement.

#### >> Wither — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**2 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Inflict Feeble 2 and Stun 1. Requires Attunement.

#### >>> Realign Biomass — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**4 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Target yourself or someone you can physically touch and are Attuned to. The target heals 2d6 HP and is cleansed of Bleeding. They suffer Stun 1.

#### >>> Bodily Sculpting — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**20 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Create a new body part of any shape which may be attached to a living creature or reshape an existing body part. Requires sufficient compatible biomass to function. For example, a human arm made of leaves would not be able to sustain itself while attached to a human, something more adjacent like animal flesh would be required. Similarly, rotten or diseased flesh would likely cause a lot of harm should it be reshaped into an organ and attached to a living creature.

#### >> Scorch — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**0 SN** · +0 ACC · -1 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Suffer Soulburn 1. Inflict Burn 2.

#### >> Flame Pylon — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · 0 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Suffer Soulburn 1. A burning target and all enemies within its A suffer Burn equal to your Soulburn.

#### >> Extinguish — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**3 Strain** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Fully cleanse your Soulburn and gain +EVA equal to its rank until your next turn.

#### >> Virulent Combustion — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**5 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor This attack can chain to enemies within C, including those it has already hit. The number of times it can chain is limited to your soulburn rank. Inflict Burn 2.

#### >> />>> Heartburn — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**5/2 SN** · -4 ACC · 0 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor This attack gains +4 ACC on burning targets. Every turn that this attack is channeled, you suffer Soulburn 1 and you inflict Burn equal to your soulburn rank on your target. If the attack is successful on the initial cast, it will continue to successfully hit on the following turns regardless of rolls. Damage that would be inflicted through ACC overflow is instead converted to additional Burn.

#### >> Ghostly Flicker — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**0 SN** · +0 ACC  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma This ability inflicts SN instead of DMG. If SN would go over the target’s SL the ability also deals equal DMG and inflicts Stun 2.

#### >> Disrupt Mind — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma The target cannot repeat any actions they used during their previous turn (CL 14 Willpower save). Inflict Stun 3.

#### >> Asylum — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**0 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Heal any amount of SN from allies within your M. You suffer that much SN divided by 2 and rounded down.

#### >> Eclipse — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Temporarily suppress the consciousness of a creature strongly enough it disappears from reality for one combat round. Only usable on creatures with half or more of their SL who were not targeted by this ability last round.

#### >> /> Void Anchor — *Channeled Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4/2 SN** · +0 ACC  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma This ability inflicts SN instead of DMG. If SN would go over the target’s SL the ability also deals equal DMG and inflicts Stun 1. Suppress consciousness in a M radius around you. Perform as a regular attack upon using the ability. While channeled on later turns, the ability acts as an aura that inflicts 3 SN to your enemies within its range for every round they spend within it.

#### >> Compress Matter — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**0 SN** · +0 ACC · 0 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Move your Mass Origin (MO) to the location of your target and Inflict Break 1. If the MO is already on your target, this attack inflicts DMG.

#### >> Lockdown — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**3 SN** · +0 ACC · 2 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst If your MO is on the target, they are unable to move until your next turn and suffer Stun 2. Otherwise, this ability costs 1 ACP and you move your MO onto the target and have it follow them until your next turn.

#### >> Repulse — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**6 SN** · +0 ACC · 2 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Inflict Push 2 in any direction on all targets within C of your MO. The targets suffer Stun 2 and 1 DMG that ignores armour if they would hit something or are instead knocked Prone (Cl 12 Stance save) if they end up in an empty space.

#### >> /> Spatial Anomaly — *Channeled Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**3/2 SN** · +0 ACC  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst You may move your MO to a place you can see within your M upon casting this ability. Inflict Stun 1 and Pull 1 towards your MO on all enemies within C of it every turn this ability remains channeled.

#### >>> Pressure Absolute — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**2 SN** · +0 ACC · +2 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst May target any number of targets within M for +2 SN for each target beyond the first. For each target choose Lift or Bend. They are affected by this ability until your next turn. Your MO disappears after performing this ability. Lift: The enemy is lifted a single tile above ground and held in place, suffering -5 EVA. Inflict Stun 2. Bend: The enemy is crushed by pressure from above and held in place Prone. Inflict Break 2.
