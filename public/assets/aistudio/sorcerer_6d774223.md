# Sorcerer

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 122-125 -->  
**Archetype:** Mystic · Wildfire · SelfBurn  
**Recommended disciplines:** Zeal/Mysticism  
**Recommended special skills:** Medicine, Discipline, Whisper, Authority  

> His parents woke him and his sister up in the middle of the night in a panicked hurry. They barely had time to dress before they were dragged out of their house and into the dark woods, alone. As they ran they could hear screams behind them and see a pyre where their home once stood. After hours of travel they collapsed in a secluded den, holding hands as they shook from the cold. He berated himself for not bringing a lighter, matches, anything to keep the cold at bay. At some point during the night their hands lit ablaze. When they woke up in the forest with their oil and torches gone they thought they wouldn’t last a day longer. Their scorchblades could provide them some light, yes, but not enough to weather the endless nights. It was precisely in the moment where they stood surrounded with cold, burnt out blades in their hands that she ignited. She would give her flesh to be their beacon in the final stand. As fire spread throughout her, their blades ignited once more and even began to spew fire on the beasts assaulting them. The miracle of her charred flesh gave them time, but more importantly it gave them hope. What remained of her body was brought back to the city and placed in a casket on a public square where she would forever watch over the central pyre. Throughout the night he stood on the castle tower bombarding the battlements below with fire fueled by his flesh. The sparks flew and jumped as if they had a will of their own, spreading through the enemy forces almost like a bolt of lightning. Throughout the night he saw many of his friends fall and the parts of the various halls he walked now bloodied and ravaged. Throughout the night he treated his flesh and did all in his power to stop it from fully burning out, yet his body was nearing its limit. The final nail was not that he lacked the will to die for the cause or that he had no body left to burn. The final blow was the realization that his sacrifice here never even mattered, that no matter his power he would never have been able to protect them. His fire was finally snuffed out, and his half intact body collapsed on the floor, not sure what to even think about in its final moments. Sorcerers are powerful channelers of flame capable of igniting their flesh and controlling the fire this produces. They excel at ravaging hordes of fleshy beings, though with more precision they can also act as supportive leaders who provide their allies with lit weaponry, cauterize their wounds as they appear and light the paths they walk. All of this is paid for in full by their bodies as their flesh degrades from repeated burns, leaving them marked by their devotion to Vamor.

## Class Features
#### Beacon of Sacrifice — *Passive*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 0*
**Effect:** You may suffer SN instead of VG damage inflicted by Soulburn. Reaching Soulburn 5 grants you a temporary destiny point that lasts until the end of the encounter, once per combat encounter.

#### >> Engine of Consumption — *Utility*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor You do not suffer Soulburn DMG during your next turn. Choose any option an amount of times equal to your Soulburn rank: Spark: Inflict Burn 1 on a target within your C. Soothe: Heal 1 SN to a target ally within your C.

#### > Flaming Rebirth — *Utility*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 2*
**0 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Restore 8 SN as long as your SN is at half or above your SL max. You suffer Fractured 1 until the end of your next turn and Feeble 2 until the end of the turn following that one.

## Path: Arsonist
*Path that removes all safeguards and fully immerses itself in the wildfires it creates.*

#### Blazing Artillery — *Passive*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 2*
**Effect:** Engine of Consumption costs you only 1 AP to perform and its Spark option has a range of M and it no longer freezes the Soulburn DMG during your next turn. Witnessing an enemy be incapacitated by a Vamor ability or Burn restores 2 VG and 2 SN to you.

#### > Ignition — *Utility*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 3*
**0 Strain**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Spend any amount of SN to suffer that much Soulburn. If you spend SN this way, gain +2 EVA until your next turn.

#### | Wildfire — *Utility*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 4*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Take another turn after this one. Usable only if your Soulburn is at rank 6 or higher. Costs 1 Destiny Point per use beyond the first in a combat encounter.

## Path: Pyreon
*A path that empowers those who would witness it, using flames to elevate their allies in various ways.*

#### Example Eternal — *Passive*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 2*
**Effect:** Engine of Consumption’s Soothe option has a range of M. Vamor Mysticism abilities you perform that target only you may be used on up to 3 total targets within your M whenever you perform them. Whenever you witness an ally under the influence of your Vamor Mysticism abilities incapacitate an enemy, restore 2 VG and 2 SN.

#### >> Heat Redistribution — *Defensive*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 3*
**6 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Heal an amount of VG equal to your Soulburn rank distributed amongst any number of allies within your C. You may spend a Destiny Point while performing this ability to extend the range to M and double the VG available. ●

#### < Inner Persistance — *Utility*
*Requires: Sorcerer Rank 4*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Triggers on you suffering DMG. Instead of taking that DMG, suffer that much Soulburn instead.
