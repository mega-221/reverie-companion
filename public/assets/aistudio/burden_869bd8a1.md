# Burden

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 31-31 -->

Burden is a measure of how cumbersome it is to function while carrying items. The
burden value of different items is listed in their stats. The intent behind this
mechanic is not to force you to meticulously track your inventory but rather to
limit your actual combat loadout to something reasonable. Burden can largely be
ignored in situations where your character is not in a time sensitive or dangerous
scene or doesn’t explicitly need to always carry all of their items. The maximum
burden a character can carry is determined by the following formula:
Burden
[ 6+GRT+Endurance+2*Carry ]
Beyond this threshold, the character suffers -1 on all rolls that use GRT or REF and
all combat rolls for every point of burden above it.
Should a character reach -10 on rolls via burden penalties, they can no longer move.
Armour Burden
The burden of worn armour is reduced by 1 for each rank the character has in the
carry skill. This is only applied while the armour is worn.
The Purpose of Burden
Burden should not be something you have to manage often, it should be there to
make you consider what weapons, armour and equipment you are carrying into
combat. This makes burden act not as a real penalty, but rather as a loadout limiter,
punishing you only if you can’t decide what you should be carrying around.
