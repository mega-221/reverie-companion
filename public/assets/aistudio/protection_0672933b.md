# Protection

<!-- chapter: 06-disciplines | pages: 145-148 -->  
**Become invulnerable, protect the innocent.**  
*Recommended for heavy armour users, evasive scoundrels and those hoping to avoid death.*  

## Abilities

#### > Evasive Footwork — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain +1 EVA until your next turn.

#### > On Guard — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain 3 TVG that last until your next turn.

#### >> Block — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Shield*
**0 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Restore 1 AP of your shield.

#### >> Shielded Attack — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Shield*
**0 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Gains +ACC equal to the sum of armour points and EVA provided by the shield.

#### > Shield Bash — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**2 SN** · +0 ACC · 2 DMG  
**Effect:** Inflict Stun 1.

#### >> Impervious — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**0 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Heal 2 SN for each attack made against you and each attack that does no DMG to you until your next turn. Only heals SN while at half or less of SL.

#### >> Elusive Target — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**1 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Gain EVA equal to your Dexterity/2, rounded down until the end of your turn.

#### >> Durable Target — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**1 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Your AP are increased by your Endurance/2, rounded down until the end of your turn.

#### >> Unpredictable Navigation — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**3 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Gain +2 tiles of movement on your next movement action. Not usable while wearing medium or heavy armour.

#### >> Fortress — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 2 · Weapons: Shield*
**3 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** You and an ally are treated as standing in cover one level higher until your next turn for as long as you are adjacent.

#### > Field Fortification — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**5 SN**  
**Effect:** Restore 1 AP. You may restore an AP and gain +2 EVA until your next turn if you spend +1 ACP to perform this ability. Not usable without worn armour or a shield that provides AP.

#### >> Slip Out — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**2 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** You cannot be targeted by attackers who haven’t attacked you since your last turn until your next turn. You cannot use this ability on consecutive turns. Not usable while wearing medium or heavy armour.

#### < Intervention — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on adjacent enemy attempting to perform an average or slower action. If you have a higher or equal REF, prevent that action.

#### < Safeguard — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an ally adjacent to you being hit by an attack. You may redirect that attack to yourself.

#### >> High Stance — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 3 · Weapons: Shield, 2H Melee Weapon*
**3 SN** · +4 EVA  
**Effect:** Bleeding, Stun and Break you suffer until your next turn are reduced by 3. Taunt a target enemy (CL 14 Willpower save).

#### < Damage Control — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an attack dealing DMG to you. Prevent up to half of that DMG rounded down after accounting for armour. This ability is only usable while wearing medium or heavy armour.

#### >> Steel Shepard — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** All attacks targeting allies standing within C of you are redirected to you as long as you are adjacent to the attackers or standing between the attackers and your allies.

#### < Split-Second Slip — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an enemy projectile attack targeting you. Gain +4 EVA against that attack. Unusable while wearing medium or heavy armour.

#### >> The Stand — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Until your next turn you gain a free reaction melee attack every time an enemy enters your A that has +0 ACC and +0 DMG (This attack does not consume your reaction for the turn).

#### >> Shielded Charge — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 4 · Weapons: Shield*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Perform a movement action going in a straight line while performing this attack. This attack is made against all targets standing inside the line of movement and the targets are pushed to the sides as they are hit. This attack gains +ACC equal to the sum of armour points and EVA provided by the shield. Lose all shield benefits until your next turn.
