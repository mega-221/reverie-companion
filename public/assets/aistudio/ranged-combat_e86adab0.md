# Ranged Combat

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 30-30 -->

In ranged combat further modifiers are often applied to your combat rolls
depending on the target’s position:
Distance:
Too close: -3 ACC
Effective range: +0 ACC
Too far: -2 ACC
Target Cover:
No cover: +0 ACC
Half cover: -1 ACC
¾ cover: -2 ACC
Full cover: -3 ACC
Speed:
Stationary: +0 ACC
Fast (speed above 4 tiles/round): -1 ACC
Incredibly fast (e.g. dragon): -3 ACC
Range Zones
All weapons have their effective range zones which are described as:
Adjacent (1 tile)
Close (2-3 tiles)
Medium (4-7 tiles)
Long (8-12 tiles)
Very Long (13+ tiles)
For one-handed thrown weapons, assume the optimal ranges to be Close and
Medium unless stated otherwise. For two-handed weapons, assume it to be just
Close.
