CH I. Introduction to Reverie
Have you ever stepped into the planar edge? It feels like standing on the border
between dream and wake. It is a place where impossible landscapes begin to form
before your eyes while your deeply held fears manifest behind your back. There is
nothing out there but your thoughts waiting to be given form. We’ve lived in our little
bubble for centuries, enough to almost forget there was once more to the world than
this tiny pocket of misery would have you believe. One thing is for certain though –
once the Purities set their minds to finally exploring what lies beyond, they will either
force their way through the veil or kill us all in the attempt.
