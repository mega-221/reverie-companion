#### > Bloodletting — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Hemomancy*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Lose 1 VG. Gain +2 ACC on your next attack.

#### > Manifest Flesh — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0, no Conviction · Failing: Any rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** above rank 1 Costs 1 DP to perform. Create one of the following attached to your body: ● Arm: gain +2 ACC ● Leg: gain +1 Tile of Movement Speed and +1 EVA ● Other Body Part: grants a Narrator defined skill bonus The effects of this ability last for 10 minutes or 3 combat rounds.

#### < Blindspot — *Utility*
