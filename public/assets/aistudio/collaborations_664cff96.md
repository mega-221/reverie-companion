# Collaborations

<!-- chapter: 02-playing-the-game | pages: 17-17 -->

Collaborations are skill checks that involve multiple people all participating at the
same time. Their individual tasks may all be the same, for example, when opening a
heavy door, or different, like when assisting a medical operation.
In those situations, the average CL of the grand task should be chosen and then
multiplied for each participant. When making the skill rolls, the scores of the
participants are pooled together and compared against the multiplied CL.
