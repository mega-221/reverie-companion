# Marksman

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 66-69 -->  
**Archetype:** Champion · Sniper · AreaControl  
**Recommended disciplines:** Precision, Protection  
**Recommended special skills:** Scout, Interface, Engineering, Pilot  

> When his village was overrun by the tall, slender stalkers, most of his neighbours tried to fight them. While some managed to wound a few, they were quickly torn apart and strung up on the nearby trees. As he watched all this from his attic hiding spot, a crossbow beside him, he fought and pleaded with his heart to show restraint, to show patience. When one of the stalkers inspected his house and concluded there was nobody inside, it settled down below his hiding spot and entered a sort of stasis. Before him was now a simple shot that would give him a chance at surviving this night, bought by his refusal to submit to a more primal response. As the sun was nearing its zenith, the contestants readied their rifles. Her engraved antique shined as bright as ever when the starting shot was issued. A plume of smoke erupted from her firearm as she executed the first target. The recoil felt right, just the way she always practiced. Not a second later, she had already rotated the weapon’s barrel mechanism and was ready to fire again. In what felt like a sudden and sharp blink, all three targets were felled from a hundred meters’ distance. She claimed her aim got better the further away the targets were, that distance allowed her to focus more on the target rather than the person. He drew his bow and turned around the corner, pointing it towards someone he thought was his nemesis. Instead his gaze was met by a frightened child, inexplicably and shakily holding a dragon pistol in her hands. He tried to defuse the situation, yet her fear was stronger. He pleaded with her to resolve this peacefully, yet she wouldn’t trust him. Having held the bow drawn for so long, his arms began to strain and he made a rare choice to show restraint in hopes of avoiding the slaughter of a child. As he was about to point the bow away she mistook his movement for an assault, discharging a blast of shrapnel towards him. This scene would be burned deep into his mind when his eyes were torn apart by the bits of deformed metal. Marksmen are proficient hunters, archers and gunners. Marksmen often choose a type of ranged weapon to stick to above others, allowing them to reach unparalleled levels of precision. When they settle into their preferred armament, they will either dedicate fully to accurate, long distance shots combined with shotcalling or adapt a more mobile hit-and-run strategy. Good marksmen are a backbone of any competent or otherwise combat unit and are feared by all melee combatants.

## Class Features
#### Empowered Shot — *Passive*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 0*
**Effect:** You gain 1 Empowered Shot Charge whenever you perform a ranged attack. Upon reaching the maximum of 3 Charges, you may spend them while performing any ranged attack in order to empower it: Volley: Repeat this attack at no Strain cost. Refresh: This attack gains +2 ACC. After performing it, heal 6 Strain if you are at half or above of your SL and reload your currently equipped weapon.

#### >> Prepare Shot — *Defensive*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 1*
**1 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** During your next turn, your first attack gains +X ACC where X is your class rank.

#### >> Overwatch — *Utility*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Until your next turn, gain 3 additional reactions that can be used as a ranged attack that has -1 ACC and +0 DMG. These reactions trigger upon enemy movement within your line of sight.

## Path: Sharpshot
*Path that specializes in accurate, long range shots and team support through target painting.*

#### Headhunter — *Passive*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever your ranged attack hits a target for 4 or more above their EVA, heal 2 Strain and gain a stacking +1 ranged DMG bonus that lasts until the end of the encounter. Empowered Shot gains the following option at 2 Charges: Judgement: Your next attack gains +2 ACC, +3 DMG and Pierce 3.

#### > Tracking Shot — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 3*
**1 SN** · +0 ACC · -3 DMG  
**Effect:** Regardless of the attack's success, you and your allies gain +1 ACC against the target for 2 rounds. This effect can reach a total of +2 ACC.

#### < Repel The Savages — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 4*
**0 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Triggers on enemy entering your M range.

## Path: Venator
*Mobile path that excels at hit-and-run tactics, being able to rapidly redeploy while firing.*

#### Frontline Assault — *Passive*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever you perform a ranged attack targeting an enemy within your C, gain +1 Empowered shot Charge. Empowered Shot gains the following option at 2 Charges: Tempo: Gain +2 EVA until your next turn. Gain an additional movement action.

#### < Return Fire — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 3*
**2 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Triggers on a ranged attack being used against you.

#### >> Combined Offensive — *Melee + Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Marksman Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Perform this as two attacks in any order - one melee and one ranged. You may use your movement action in between them. You may freely holster weapons in between them.
