Wardens are heavily armored fighters serving as the frontline of any force they are a part of. Often trained in more diplomatic approaches, their aim is to prevent conflict first and defend second. If their menacing presence doesn’t defuse the situation by itself, their prowess and brutality eventually will.

## Class Features
#### Iron Golem — *Passive*
*Requires: Warden Rank 0*
**Effect:** Whenever one of your armour points are broken, you gain 3 TVG. Whenever you restore an armour point, you gain +1 DMG until your next turn.

#### < Bodyguard — *Utility*
*Requires: Warden Rank 1*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an ally within your movement distance being targeted by an attack. You move next to the ally, exchange places with them and change the target of the attack to yourself if it is a melee attack. If it is a ranged attack, you instead move at any point between the attacker and the targeted ally and change the target of the attack to yourself. Restore 1 armour point.

#### >> Invite Punishment — *Defensive*
*Requires: Warden Rank 2*
**2 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Taunt up to two target enemies (CL 14 Willpower save). Restore 3 Strain for each enemy you successfully taunt this way.

## Path: Juggernaut
*Path that grows more destructive as it loses armour.*

#### Unburdened — *Passive*
*Requires: Warden Rank 2*
**Effect:** You may choose to deplete all of your armour points at the start of your turn. If you do, gain a temporary destiny point that lasts until the end of that turn.

#### >> Regain Composure — *Defensive*
*Requires: Warden Rank 3*
**3 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Regain 2 armour points if you have no armour points. Restore 7 Strain if you have more than half of your SL.

#### >> Locomotive — *Melee Attack*
*Requires: Warden Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +2 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Perform an additional movement action. You may pass through enemies as you perform it and knock them and the attack’s target prone (CL 14 Stance save).

## Path: Sentinel
*Intimidation focused path that spread terror throughout the battlefield.*

#### Gaze and Tremble — *Passive*
*Requires: Warden Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever you Taunt or Intimidate an enemy, you may instead inflict the other of the effects. Instead of having a duration, those status effects last until the afflicted enemy successfully deals DMG to you. Deal +1 Stun and +1 Break against targets you have Taunted or Intimidated.

#### > Tremendous Presence — *Defensive*
*Requires: Warden Rank 3*
**3 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Until your next turn, enemies you have Taunted or Intimidated suffer your choice of the following: ● -2 ACC ● -2 EVA ● -1 Tile of Movement Speed

#### > Opress — *Melee Attack*
*Requires: Warden Rank 4*
**3 SN** · +0 ACC · -3 DMG  
**Effect:** Inflict Stun 2 and Break 2.
