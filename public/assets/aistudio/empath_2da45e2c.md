# Empath

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 118-121 -->  
**Archetype:** Mystic · Strain · Divination  
**Recommended disciplines:** Zeal/Mysticism  

> After a battle would end, she would arrive. To tend to the survivors’ wounds, but more importantly to their souls. No matter the side, no matter the goal, no matter the thanks, she would comfort each and every living and dying soul, easing their mental toll and letting them return to their regular lives. She would take in all of this pain and dissolve it within herself, becoming a tiny bit more hollow each time, becoming a tiny bit more capable each time, becoming a tiny bit more nobody each time. He knew of both the dangers and boons of the planar edge. He’d spent his life studying it, wandering the void in between, in search of things forgotten and things yet to be learned. By the time they accused him of dark magic he was already long gone from the town. By the time the brigands shot at him he was already prepared to dodge each and every arrow sent his way in an arrangement of movements no human could ever attempt. By the time the axe split his head, he had already long made peace with his inevitable fate. Armed with naught but a walking stick, she barged into the target’s house. The coward tried to ambush her, yet his arms flickered and his sword fell through them to the ground. “Did you murder them?” - she asked in an unfeeling tone. Before the accused could even open his mouth, his mind had already betrayed him, spilling out all that went through it. Satisfied with the answer, she declared her judgement. The coward’s body slumped to the ground, his mind detaching from the person, aimlessly staring at the floor. In but a minute the coward would flicker away for the last time, disappearing not just from reality but from the minds of those who knew him. She never felt bad about what she had to do, in part because she thought oblivion preferable to corporeal punishment, in part because her memory of them would soon expire. Empaths are diviners and telepaths capable of previewing the thoughts and emotions of living creatures as well as severely dampening them. Some empaths fully dedicate themselves to interpretation of creatures and are able to predict their actions in advance while others prefer to hone their ability to disrupt the stream of consciousness, becoming capable of fully erasing living beings from existence. While these abilities are powerful, empaths are often limited in their use due to their devotion to the ideals of Bruma, making them possibly the least destructive type of mystic.

## Class Features
#### Laid Bare — *Passive*
*Requires: Empath Rank 0*
**Effect:** At the beginning of your turn in combat you may choose a creature with PSY equal to or lower than yours. You learn the actions that will be performed by the chosen creature during their next turn.

#### >> Vast Soul — *Defensive*
*Requires: Empath Rank 1*
**0 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Heal SN equal to your class rank plus your highest Bruma conviction rank. An ally you can see gains +EVA equal to your class rank. Only usable at half or more of your maximum SL.

#### < Ephemera — *Utility*
*Requires: Empath Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Triggers on enemy performing an action in combat that requires a roll. You may reduce the roll by 2.

## Path: Prophet
*A path that emphasizes the connection to the planar void and the sudden bursts of knowledge it bestows upon its acolytes.*

#### Fate Foretold — *Passive*
*Requires: Empath Rank 2*
**Effect:** Once per day, you may roll 3d6 and note down the result. During that day, you may replace any roll you witness with the noted result.

#### > Predetermination — *Utility*
*Requires: Empath Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma If one exists, give the target of an ability predicted through Laid Bare +2 EVA against that attack.

#### | Fate Rejected — *Utility*
*Requires: Empath Rank 4*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Activate after any roll. Increase a failing by 2 ranks or gain a new failing at rank 2. You gain a number of temporary destiny points equal to your DP maximum that can be spent in any way on that roll. Those destiny points disappear after the roll.

## Path: Specter
*A path that focuses on erasure of consciousness and Strain manipulation.*

#### Void Brand — *Passive*
*Requires: Empath Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever you inflict SN upon a creature, that creature becomes Branded until the end of combat or for 2 hours outside of combat. You gain +2 on all PSY and Bruma ability rolls against Branded creatures.

#### >> Self Implosion — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Empath Rank 3*
**+0 SN** · +0 ACC  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma The target’s non-defensive abilities cost +2 SN until your next turn (CL 15 Willpower Save). While this effect persists, the target suffers DMG equal to the SN it inflicts upon itself via ability usage.

#### >> Annul — *Utility*
*Requires: Empath Rank 4*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Target creature suffers DMG equal to their current SN (CL 15 Willpower save).
