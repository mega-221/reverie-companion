# Witch

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 126-129 -->  
**Archetype:** Mystic · Fleshweaving · Reanimation  
**Recommended disciplines:** Zeal/Mysticism  

> She burst into the dimly lit cellar with only a purple flower in hand. On a central table there was a beggar, his leg amputated in order to evoke more pity from the passersby. Around him, two grotesque figures stood, instruments of pain in hand and grimaces in place of what would be their faces. She took a whiff of the flower and attuned to the torturer’s bodies, analysing their bones and muscles. Seconds later, they would fall to the ground, puke erupting from their orifices, blood trickling from their flayed skin. All that was left to restore the balance was to mend the beggar’s appendage back where it belonged. The winter after her husband’s death had almost killed her. Isolated in that forest, she could barely sustain herself on berries and the pitiful produce of her garden. In desperation, she turned to her late husband’s study in search of a solution. While she was shocked to learn of the true nature of his research, it would prove to be her salvation. She exhumed his corpse and raised a revenant of flesh, just like his journal had explained. With tears in her eyes and peace in her heart, she would order “it” to hunt. They all hated him and they all relied on him to survive. He began to wonder if his presence had made his comrades’ instincts duller, their recklessness worse. They would break formation to assault beasts head on, knowing he would quickly reattach any appendage or organ they lost. They charged into fire fully expecting him to regrow their skin once the dust had settled. He was a useful freak, a monster capable of unmaking or remaking life, but a freak they could no longer function without. Witches are occult healers and mediums versed in the arts of fleshweaving and necromancy. Some witches place a greater importance on manipulation of flesh and the living, acting as potent grafters or inflicting great harm upon skin and bone. Other witches work to perfect their mastery and control over the dead, being able to temporarily or permanently recall souls into their decaying bodies and bend their actions to their will. Due to their devotion to Halice, witches are often radical individuals bent on shaping the world into a new form. Even the corpses of those who opposed them will rise again to serve those ideals.

## Class Features
#### Rhythm of Life — *Passive*
*Requires: Witch Rank 0*
**Effect:** When performing a Survival or Medicine check on a creature, you may gain Strain to increase your result by that much up to a maximum of +2. Halice abilities that require Attunement can be performed without it and apply it to their targets after being performed.

#### >> Fleshbound Hex — *Utility*
*Requires: Witch Rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 3 targets within your Attunement network, remove them from the network and inflict one of the following upon them: Flay Skin: Inflict Bleeding 2. Grind Bones: Inflict Stun 2. The target cannot move until the end of their next turn (CL 18 Stance save). Spoil Humors: Inflict Feeble 2. The target suffers +1 DMG from all sources until the end of your next turn.

#### >> Emergency Binding — *Defensive*
*Requires: Witch Rank 2*
**4 SN** · +1 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Target yourself or an ally. The target restores VG equal to the VG they lost since their previous turn +1. Each consecutive use of this ability also inflicts Bleeding equal to the number of times the ability has been used.

## Path: Occultist
*A path that ventures further into precise flesh manipulation, providing potent wards to allies and devastating curses to its enemies.*

#### Flesh Ward — *Passive*
*Requires: Witch Rank 2*
**Effect:** Allies within your Attunement network gain 2 TVG whenever you perform Fleshbound Hex. You may perform Vital attunement at only 1 AP cost by suffering 3 SN.

#### < Flaying Snare — *Utility*
*Requires: Witch Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an ally within your Attunement network being damaged. The attacker is added to your Attunement network and they suffer Bleeding 2.

#### | Sensory Overload — *Utility*
*Requires: Witch Rank 4*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Target up to 8 creatures. They are considered a part of your Attunement network until the end of your turn.

## Path: Necromancer
*A path that empowers the reanimated dead and utilizes corpses in gruesome ways.*

#### Repurposed Servants — *Passive*
*Requires: Witch Rank 2*
**Effect:** You may control up to two Zombies at the same time. Zombies you control do not suffer ACC penalties from being reanimated.

#### > Splinter Spider — *Utility*
*Requires: Witch Rank 3*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Target a Zombie you control that’s next to an enemy target and has full AP. The Zombie latches onto the target (CL 16 Stance Save) and, if the target fails to resist them, uses their ribcage and other available bones as a makeshift bear trap, performing a +0 ACC 4 DMG attack that cannot miss and inflicts Bleeding 3.

#### >> Sack Rapture — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Witch Rank 4*
**3 SN** · +2 ACC · +3 DMG  
**Effect:** Must target a corpse. This attack affects the C range of the corpse. The corpse explodes, inflicting Push 1, Fractured 2 and Stun 2 on those caught within the blast.
