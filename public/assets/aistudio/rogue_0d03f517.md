> When her mother disappeared she tried her best to make it work. She offered herself for all sorts of work, yet most would reject her for her nearly skeletal appearance. How was she to feed her baby brother without money? A loaf of bread stolen here, some jewelry snatched in a busy street there – sometimes she felt like she could really make it work. Other times they’d go a week without food, becoming more jaded with the indifference of the world, becoming more open to depraved acts. She went over this in her head a thousand times before torching those fields, consulting her heart. She would take what was rightfully hers, they left her no other choice. The newspaper spoke of a legendary marauder being finally put in his place by the heroic militias. He was in the business of offering “protection” to hamlets and villages, aided by his countless demonstrations of why protection was needed. The tyrant ruled the region with an iron fist for a decade, and nobody could or bothered to muster enough firepower to repel his minions. The twist lay in the fact that he had not perished due to the heroism of small people, but a personal and deeply uncharacteristic blunder. In his final year, he had fallen madly in love with a local woman, going as far as to conceive a child. He would cut corners from his vast empire, all to fund a majestic homestead for his new family. After months of infiltrating this undersupplied network, the militias had finally caught wind of the location of his family home. The article concluded by jubilantly describing the remnants of that tragic scene, ending it by mockingly referring to the deceased as being “killed by sentimentality”. She wiped the fresh blood from her blade. This night's work had been the final point in her grand plan, and with a little luck, this may very well have been her last kill. The six of them hadn’t even noticed she snuck into the building, and with her swift movements she made sure to both secure her position at the top of the city’s underground and to eliminate the final person who could identify her. The moon was full that night, and a slight breeze signaled that it was the perfect time to enjoy the sights of the grand metropolis. She climbed one of the ruined towers, making a final mental check, confirming she was materially set for life. Reaching the top she made herself comfortable on one of the overgrown edges of the railing, picking up a white flower that grew there. She smelled it and looked over her city, content, happy for the first time since she could remember. Gazing over the families enjoying the evening she felt her heart skip a beat. Spotting some couples out on a walk made her suddenly feel cold. She quickly shook it off and went back down, assuring herself that ghosts are best left unseen. Rogues are chaotic and resourceful operatives and swords for hire. They excel in ambushes and other underhanded tactics, preferring to make their own luck instead of wasting time on concepts like training and discipline. Distractions are a rogue’s favorite weapon, be they loud and explosive or more subtle and elegant. Rogues come from various molds, ranging from simple highwaymen and bandits to eloquent members of high society.

## Class Features
#### Veil of Shadows — *Passive*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 0*
**Effect:** Gain advantage on rolls being performed in Stealth while near creatures who would be hostile to this action. You may enter Stealth whenever you use a defensive action while in full cover and out of enemies’ lines of sight. While in Stealth, the cost of abilities you perform is reduced by 2 Strain.

#### > Wicked Glint — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 1 · Weapons: Stealth*
**2 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack suffers no penalties for being performed at A range. If you have a free hand, you may use holstered weapons to perform this attack.

#### >> Swift Resupply — *Defensive*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 2*
**2 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** When performing this ability in cover, choose two of the following: ● Restore 1 VG ● Restore 1 SN ● Freely holster and equip weapons ● Gain +1 EVA until your next turn ● Use or apply a consumable to your equipment

## Path: Brigand
*Path that grows bolder in its risk-taking, preferring to continue the fight after an explosive entrance.*

#### Stacked Sleeve — *Passive*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 2*
**Effect:** You may use Pistol and consumables with Wicked Glint. Whenever you perform a defensive action, you may reload any equipped weapon by paying 1 Strain.

#### >> Explosive Deterrent — *Defensive*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 3*
**3 SN** · +1 EVA  
**Effect:** Use an explosive consumable and gain a free movement action.

#### | Last Drop — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 4*
**3 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Only usable against targets who have 2 or less VG.

## Path: Phantom
*Elusive path that weaves in and out of combat, cleanly eliminating its enemies one by one.*

#### Flawless Misdirection — *Passive*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 2*
**Effect:** You may enter Stealth via the effects of Veil of Shadows while in ¾ cover. Wicked glint may be performed with no bonus DMG from ACC overflow without breaking Stealth.

#### > Shocking Display — *Utility*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Your next attack while in Stealth inflicts Stun 2 on up to two targets within your target’s M. This effect lasts until your next turn.

#### >>> Silent Elimination — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Rogue Rank 4*
**6 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +8 DMG against Unaware targets. If it kills the target while you are in Stealth, you remain in Stealth. Usable only with quiet weapons.
