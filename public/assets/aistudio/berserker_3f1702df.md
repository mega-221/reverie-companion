# Berserker

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 58-61 -->  
**Archetype:** Champion · Risky · Brutal  
**Recommended disciplines:** Warfare  
**Recommended special skills:** Stance, Carry, Authority, Discipline  

> The guard had been mocking him for the past hour. Having drunk a sufficient quantity of absinthe, he was ready to return the favour. First, he swung his crude mace directly at his plated chest, denting the armour plates towards him and breaking his ribcage. As the guard fell to his knees, gasping for air, he aligned his arms for another swing, blowing apart the guard’s horrified, gasping face onto the nearby wall, just like his father had taught him. When the townsfolk found him, he was quickly subdued and they prepared a nearby tree for a mob lynching. As he was hanged for his crimes, for the first time in his life, he would ponder the downsides of violence. He rapidly rose through the ranks, earning a medal every other engagement. He was the unit’s greatest asset, his brutality, efficiency and bravery unmatched. After one such skirmish and the following alcohol-laden celebration, he would visit his lover’s tent in search of warmth. His heart would sink when she met his presence with a wide look and dilated pupils, an expression all too familiar to his murderous body. Taking her for a simple farmer, the lowlives had surrounded her and were already throwing about nasty suggestions. Trying to maintain a facade of bravery, she slashed one’s leg with her father’s scythe, half-severing it. As the bandit bled on the ground she pushed her hand into the wound, licked a few drops of his blood and then smeared it all over her face. In a moment of shock she cut down the second bandit as the last one ran for the forest path they arrived from. When the screaming subsided she dropped her implement, sat down and began to quietly sob. Berserkers are simple and brutal warriors - they utilize their great strength to quickly lay waste to the enemy, usually wielding two-handed weapons and not wearing any armour that would unnecessarily slow them down. They often make use of intimidation to get their way, leading others to view them as dangerous individuals even when not wielding a weapon.

## Class Features
#### Reckless Abandon — *Passive*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 0*
**Effect:** Whenever you perform an attack, you may have it deal +X damage and suffer -X EVA until your next turn where X is your GRT+REF up to a maximum of 4.

#### >>> Breakthrough — *Melee Attack*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 1*
**4 SN** · +2 ACC · +1 DMG  
**Effect:** If this attack kills or incapacitates your target, gain an additional movement action and repeat it at no Strain cost. Breakthrough can only be repeated like this once per round.

#### | Rooted Blows — *Utility*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Lose your movement action for this turn. Breakthrough can repeat twice upon incapacitating or killing a target this round.

## Path: Revenant
*An unstoppable offensive path that excels under pressure and grows more powerful after suffering damage.*

#### Defiant Rage — *Passive*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 2*
**Effect:** Gain +1 ACC and +2 DMG while at half or lower of your HP max.

#### > Power Through — *Utility*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain 3 TVG. This effect does not stack.

#### > Tear Muscle — *Utility*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 4*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** You may lose an amount of HP less than or equal to your GRT and inflict that much additional DMG on your next attack this turn. This ability may be used in place of your movement action for the turn instead.

## Path: Ravager
*A bleed-focused path that inflicts terror in groups of enemies.*

#### Bloodbath — *Passive*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever you inflict Bleeding, inflict equal Stun to enemies within C of the target (not the target themselves).

#### > Bloody Revelry — *Utility*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Intimidate all bleeding enemies within your line of sight (CL 3 Willpower save). Until your next turn, roll your attacks against them with advantage.

#### >>> Spill Guts — *Melee Attack*
*Requires: Berserker Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack may target any number of targets and gains +1 ACC for each additional target. Inflict Bleeding 2.
