# Strain

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 22-22 -->

Strain (SN) is a measure of your character’s morale and exhaustion. Strain increases
whenever a character performs advanced abilities, exhausting activities or gets
otherwise demoralized or tired. Every character starts with a strain limit (SL) of 5
which increases by 1 each time they reach a new Tier. SL can be further increased
by picking the relevant advancements when leveling up. Strain is also important for
a number of different character centered systems that will be discussed in later
chapters.
Suffering Strain in Combat
Whenever your character would reach 0 or less VG, they gain SN equal to half of
their maximum SL. This can only occur once per combat encounter.
Getting Rid of Strain
There are plenty of ways to remove a character’s Strain. The most common is
through resting, but a variety of consumables and a fair number of abilities also
offer strain restoration. In addition to those, the Narrator may also determine some
strain should be lost during moments of hope and overall positive events. Examples
of such events may include:
-1 SN: succeeding an important skill check, reaching a beautiful view
-2 SN: surviving a tough encounter, reaching safe shelter
-3 SN: succeeding on a CL 18 or tougher roll,
Standing On The Edge
Should a character’s SN ever increase past their SL, their mind will be put to test
and they will have to pass a CL 18 conviction check (3d6 + chosen conviction) in
order not to suffer a crisis. Convictions and crises are fully explained in chapter 4.
Diving Into The Abyss
Should your character ever reach double their SL, they will suffer a heart attack and
be reduced to [ 1 + Endurance ] of their maximum HP and their SN will be reset
back to the maximum SL. The second heart attack will always be lethal. Should a
character wish to avoid the risk of the second heart attack, they can choose to
Accept Fate upon reaching the first one.
