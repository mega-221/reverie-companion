# Protean

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 130-133 -->  
**Archetype:** Aberration · Shapeshifter · Incoherent  
**Recommended disciplines:** Beast  

> With his sister’s death he was left truly and utterly alone. His days blended together into a single foggy timeline as he aimlessly wandered the city’s streets. Nobody was left to give him tasks, nobody was left to scold him, nobody was left to hold him while he slept on the cold cobblestone. In this dreamy state he would often imagine himself a stray cat, jumping about the various windows and poles of the metropolis. In this dreamy state, a maddened drunk would kick in his fragile, feline ribs. She had run out of pages in her journal. What was supposed to be a routine scouting expedition turned into months of lost wandering through the ever shifting and unfamiliar landscapes. She hadn’t even noticed the second pair of legs she grew when the feet of her old legs scraped off. She barely even registered that she could see everything around her using the new eyes that appeared on the sides of her head. When she miraculously returned to the planar edge, she realized she could not remember why she was searching for it to begin with. His lover’s death had made him a recluse. He wept day and night without pause, his mind dwelling on her image, on her mangled body. He cried thinking about how she must have felt in her final moments. Those who attempted to visit him would be scared away by the apparition of the late lover that dwelled within the apartment, that he had come to fully embody. His bloody tears would leave his body drop by drop, until nothing remained of him but a reddish stain on the bedroom floor. Proteans are shapeshifters who have been alienated from reality through intense psychological trauma or isolation. Their material form is unstable and they can easily manifest their imagination in the space surrounding them. Most people find them disturbing to interact with due to their often grotesque features and tendencies to mimic the forms of other people. If left unchecked for too long, they can easily get fully lost inside of their minds and completely disappear.

## Class Features
#### Dissociative Self — *Passive*
*Requires: Protean Rank 0*
**Effect:** You forgo the destiny point cost of the Beast discipline abilities. You may not raise a Conviction to a rank beyond 1 and you may not spend destiny points to add Conviction bonuses to rolls. If you reach a point where you have 3 Failings at rank 4, you will completely destabilize, get lost within your own unreality and disappear. If you reach a point where you have no more Failings, you will be cured of this condition. In addition to other costs, in order to reduce a Failing to rank 0 you must also spend 7 XP or reduce your skills to make up for the cost. When you get rid of your condition, your memories and body revert to the state they were in before you were afflicted. Your life as a protean remains in your mind as nothing but a painful, long and hazy daydream.

#### | Selfscape — *Utility*
*Requires: Protean Rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Spend 1 Destiny Point to perform this ability or increase one of your Failings by 1 rank. Gain 3 AP and an additional reaction until your next turn. The AP gained this way may only be spent on Beast and Protean abilities.

#### >> Manifest Delusion — *Defensive*
*Requires: Protean Rank 2*
**4 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** If you are not damaged until your next turn, heal 8 SN down to half of your max SL. Otherwise, gain 3 temporary destiny points that last until the end of the combat encounter and increase one of your Failings by 1 rank.

## Path: Changeling
*Path that fully utilizes its manifestation powers to warp its body into a terrifying and flexible tool.*

#### Entropic Being — *Passive*
*Requires: Protean Rank 2*
**Effect:** Abilities your perform cost 1 Strain less. Manifest Flesh and Manifest Matter last until the end of the combat encounter. You lose one of the body parts or items gained from them every time you suffer 3 or more DMG in a single attack.

#### < Imagined Flesh — *Utility*
*Requires: Protean Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on enemy attack dealing 3 or more DMG to you. Choose: Sacrifice: Destroy one of your manifested body parts to completely negate the DMG of the attack. Retaliate: Perform an immediate +2 ACC +0 DMG attack that inflicts Fractured 1 on the enemy who triggered this reaction.

#### >>> Lifeline — *Defensive*
*Requires: Protean Rank 4*
**0 SN** · +4 EVA  
**Effect:** Costs a number of destiny points equal to your DP maximum to perform. Destroy all of your manifested body parts and items and heal 2 VG and 2 SN for each of them. Reduce one of your Failings by 1 rank.

## Path: Mimic
*Path that perfects precise visual manipulation in order to mimic other creatures.*

#### Surrogate Identity — *Passive*
*Requires: Protean Rank 2*
**Effect:** When performing any kind of social roll, you may suffer up to 3 SN to gain an equal bonus for that roll. Manifest Flesh may be cast using 1 Destiny Point in order to completely change your appearance to match that of a known creature instead of its other effects.

#### | Miserable Puppet — *Utility*
*Requires: Protean Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** The creature you are taking the appearance of that is witnessing you suffers Strain whenever you suffer strain or DMG until your next turn.

#### >>> Cling to Reality — *Defensive*
*Requires: Protean Rank 4*
**0 SN** · +4 EVA  
**Effect:** Costs a number of destiny points equal to your DP maximum to perform. Abandon your disguise and lose the ability to change your appearance for 4 weeks. Reduce one of your Failings by 2 ranks.
