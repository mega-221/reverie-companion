# Skill Checks

<!-- chapter: 02-playing-the-game | pages: 12-13 -->

When playing the game, you will often be faced with tasks that require a specific
skill set to be completed. In these instances, you will perform a skill check. Each
skill check will consist of rolling 3d6, adding them up and then further adding the
values of a relevant skill your character possesses, as determined by the Narrator.
For each rank you have in the required attribute and skill you will add +1 to the final
result. If the final result matches or exceeds the assigned challenge level (CL) you
will have succeeded the skill check.
Radiant Success
When performing a skill check, a radiant success is achieved when the result of a
roll is higher than the required CL by at least 4. When you achieve a radiant
success, your character will perform the task with exceptional ease, inspiring hope
and restoring 3 Vigor or 3 Strain as chosen by the player.
Narrative Failure
Since Reverie is a system with binary success/failure on rolls, it is important to
ensure the players are never just told that “nothing happens” after a failure. It is the
duty of the Narrator to attempt to provide the players with new rolls, situations and
complications if they fail instead of completely cutting off the game’s narrative.
