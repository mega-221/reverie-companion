# Destiny Points

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 34-34 -->

Destiny points (DP) are an expendable resource player characters have access to
that can be used in multiple ways. The default way they can be used is to spend a
DP after performing any roll in order to add +X to that roll’s result where X is the
rank of a conviction that aligns with that roll. These effects cannot be stacked or
combined on a single roll, and if done during combat they are limited to one use per
encounter. Various abilities and aspects a character has can sometimes give them
new ways to spend destiny points.
Gaining Destiny Points
Destiny points should be gained at least at the end of every session or once during
2-3 hours of play. Beyond that, once per that same time period, they should be
earnable when a character performs an action that aligns with their convictions.
Each character starts with a 2 DP maximum.
