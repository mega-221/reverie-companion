CH VI. Disciplines
This chapter contains the list of all disciplines and their abilities. While classes tend
to greatly benefit from one or two disciplines, you should also consider how more
obscure discipline choices could modify and specialize your powers. A warden
could use halice mysticism to reanimate their enemies and have a whole band of
expendable meatshields. A rogue could utilize trumst mysticism’s crows to survey
areas before infiltrating them in person. An envoy could adopt the knowledge of
echo to get a better read on their allies. A vessel could make great use of precision
abilities to initially wound their victims. There are countless possible and viable
combinations of classes and disciplines to be explored and exploited.
Available Disciplines
There are a total of 10 unique disciplines to choose from:
● Warfare - Slay your foes mercilessly, take their blows head on
● Duel - Create openings, humiliate the enemy
● Protection - Become invulnerable, protect the innocent
● Leadership - Inspire comrades, push them to their limits
● Shadow - Strike unseen, win by any means
● Precision - Eliminate from afar, oversee the battlefield
● Echo - Exploit the past, animate constructs
● Zeal - Profess your convictions, evoke your Season.
● Mysticism - Ponder your ideals, reshape the world.
● Beast - Abandon self, unleash nightmares
