# Special Skills

<!-- chapter: 02-playing-the-game | pages: 16-16 -->

Special skills are a set of 12 additional, more narrow skills that can be chosen by
individual characters who would benefit from having them. Each character may
take on up to 5 special skills in addition to core skills. Special skills can increase in
rank in the same way as core skills. They include:
GRT:
Carry - Back strength. Your ability to carry heavy items and armour over a
prolonged period.
Discipline - Nerve and courage. Your ability to maintain a clear mind under physical
pressure and duress.
Stance - Posture. your ability to grip items and avoid tripping.
REF:
Interface - Sleight of hand. Your ability to perform precise hand movements like
lockpicking.
Pilot - Systemic coordination. Your ability to operate vehicles and siege weapons.
Scout - Worldly navigation. Your ability to survey and quickly traverse areas.
INT:
Engineering - Advanced problem solving. Your ability to apply scientific principles
to complex tasks and craft items.
Lore - Studied narratives. Your knowledge of the world’s history, geography and the
humanities.
Medicine - Anatomy and herbalism. Your ability to mend injuries and brew
concoctions.
PSY:
Authority - Soft force. Your ability to impose your will onto others.
Deception - Dishonesty and composure. Your ability to lie and manipulate others.
Whisper - Planar entanglement. Your ability to interact with non-humans and the
unknowable.
