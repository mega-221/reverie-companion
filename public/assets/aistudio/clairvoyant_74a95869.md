# Clairvoyant

**Archetype:** Scholar · MemoryTheft · Prosthetics  
**Recommended disciplines:** Echo  

> The crime scene was already blocked off and detectives were swarming the premises - they were waiting for her. As she entered the dim tent, her eyes briefly scanned the room before settling on the dead body in the middle. She calmed her breathing and focused on her engraved ring, on the corpse. She felt the scene of death assemble before her and play out the exact moment the deceased cried out in fear and pain - the exact moment of his stabbing. All that was left was to sketch the face of the murderer and send it to print. His path to the vault was now blocked by only a single guard. He extended his metallic arm and calmed down. He entered his target’s memories and took a hold of the past few days. With a single snap of his fingers, he violently ripped them out, leaving behind a scattering husk of a man and a great migraine in the place his recent memories once occupied. Finally, he simply pushed the guard out of the way and proceeded to the vault. Later that day, in the safety of his hideout, he would delve into the memories he tore out. He would traverse the vignettes of workplace abuse, a crumbling family life, a lost love, culminating in that terrible, painful erasure. He felt horrible for the entire duration of 5 minutes it took for him to decide he would rather erase these memories entirely than deal with their ramifications. She sat atop the lookout tower in a bath of cool water. Her entire synthetic torso was flaring up from the effort she was putting out. She was rapidly scanning and analyzing the battlefield below, delving into each enemy’s memories, predicting their next moves and transmitting this information to her allies. She often felt more like a machine than a person, yet her efficiency was undisputed and she would rather that they find her melted metallic remnants than witness what they felt in their final moments. Clairvoyants are investigators and tacticians, utilizing ambedic artifacts and prosthetics to interact with and influence the memories of those around them. They are able to learn the fighting style of an opponent in mere seconds and can even rip memories out provided their equipment can handle it. Some clairvoyants will further opt into memory manipulation, making the process stronger and more damaging to the target’s mind while others may prefer to help out their allies through intense battlefield analysis and memory transmission, creating an almost instantaneous communication network.

## Class Features
#### Analytical Mind — *Passive*
*Requires: Clairvoyant Rank 0*
**Effect:** Whenever you Analyze a target, gain a choice of either +2 ACC or +2 EVA against them. The target stops being Analyzed when you Analyze a new target.

#### >> Memory Extraction — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Clairvoyant Rank 1*
**3 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack inflicts Stun and SN equal to the DMG it would deal and heals an equal amount of your SN. Must target an Analyzed target.

#### >> /> Overlord — *Defensive*
*Requires: Clairvoyant Rank 2*
**2 SN** · -2 EVA  
**Effect:** Grant your buffs against Analyzed targets to any number of allies.
