# Origins

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 38-50 -->

Origins are a summarized backstory of your character and are chosen during
character creation. They provide your character with a narrative background in the
world and start you out with relevant special skills or discounts towards learning
relevant core skills – inspirations. They are by no means a set template you should
follow during character creation, but rather a guideline and a suggestion.
Origins are split into generic origins available to all characters and plane origins
available only to characters from that plane. It wouldn’t make sense for a character
created for a campaign in Ulvandul to have a Vesperan origin without them having
lived there and then somehow travelled between planes. As the timeline of your
world progresses and interplanar travel becomes more accessible, this restriction
should be gradually lifted.
Keep in mind that generic origins tend to give you more freedom when it comes to
what bonuses they provide while the plane specific origins tend to be more narrow
and potent.
Generic Origins
Generic origins are available to all characters regardless of their home plane.
Commoner
Your comfortable everyday existence was turned on its head, and now you must
answer the call.
Inspiration: Any basic skill
Start with: Any special skill at rank 2
Merchant
Your life of travel gave you but a tiny preview of what was to come.
Inspiration: Persuasion, Knowledge
Start with: Lore 2

Warrior
Bloodspill has always been your true calling, yet you wonder if there could be a higher
purpose to your proficiency.
Inspiration: Athletics
Start with: Armour 2, Stance 2
Brigand
You have led a life of crime and violence in a cutthroat world bent on erasing you, yet
something made you question if this was truly your destiny.
Inspiration: Dexterity, Stealth
Start with: Interface 2
Scholar
You have spent countless nights obsessing over holes in history books and incomplete
maps. It is time you took the matter of truth into your own hands.
Inspiration: Knowledge
Start with: Lore 3

Eterion Origins
The city-plane of redemption promotes the values of self reflection and altruism above
all others. Its purities guide the steady wheel of technological progress that
encompasses a vast variety of professions while the various mystics and prophets
inhabit the forgotten half-space mists on the plane’s edges. Ambedic crystals saturate
the city in vignettes of lives long gone, of moments and emotions captured in time.
The reclamation of the planar edges is an ongoing yet slow process, and the city’s
population is starting to get too large to be supported by its meager resources. The
grand solution presented by the purities are the Marches, expeditions into the planar
void, hoping to send the best and brightest of Eterion to other planes and secure the
city’s future prosperity.
Seeker
You’ve escaped death elsewhere and wound up in the city of redemption. Limitless
opportunities stretch themselves out in front of you.
Inspiration: Willpower, Any basic skill
Start with: Any special skill 2
Artisan
You have spent your life as a well oiled, productive cog in the city’s machine. Can you
take control of your own fate?
Inspiration: Reasoning or Knowledge
Start with: Carry 2, Engineering 2
City Bureaucrat
There was only so much pettiness and bickering you could handle before abandoning
your political career. You’re sure there are other fields where your skills will be more
appreciated.
Inspiration: Intuition, Persuasion
Start with: Deception 2

Memorialist
You revel in the exploration of history and people’s fates. Form your thesis and share it
with the world.
Inspiration: Intuition, Perception
Start with: Lore 2
Fogborn
You’ve lived on the edge of the plane and have learned to blend in with the shadows of
reality. Your calm demeanor and mental fortitude guide you on your endless journey.
Inspiration: Survival
Start with: Whisper 2, Lore 2

Agmar Origins
Agmar’s dead, scorched surface and everthundering skies have pushed its inhabitants
to two extremes – while ground-dwellers carve out their existence in shade the
nomads rule the skies aboard airships and on dragonback. The conflict between these
two ways of life defines the lives of the plane’s people. The city folk slave away under
the rule of the sky clans’ immense fleets, hoping to develop some way to strike down
their monsters from the sky. The major nomad clans of Khurni, Galadri and Lakumi
play political games, dividing the plane as they choose and frequently clashing over
scarce resources.
Stormrider
The sky is your one true home, the thunder your hymn. Wherever the storm takes you
next, you will accept it with an open heart and mind.
Inspiration: Survival, Dexterity
Start with: Pilot 2
Ravineborn
You have toiled day and night to carve out your existence in the harshest of climates.
You will outlast whatever storm attempts to wash over you, be it man, lizard or
thunder.
Inspiration: Endurance, Willpower
Start with: Carry 2
Sky Raider
To you, freedom doesn’t stop where another’s ends. Your countless victims were free to
resist you, you were free to pilfer them, and your desire for freedom won out.
Inspiration: Brawling
Start with: Stance 2, Pilot 2
Wastelander
Most would think you mad for choosing to live in the various crevices and tunnels of
the agmarian wastelands. Maybe they’re right, but at least your torturous existence
has taught you a trick or two about surviving in places where it shouldn’t be possible.
Inspiration: Survival, Endurance
Start with: Carry 2

Steam Artificer
You have studied long and hard to end up at the forefront of innovation. Your
knowledge of airships, thermal energy and steam engines is rivaled by none.
Inspiration: Reason
Start with: Engineering 2, Lore 2

Vesperik Origins
Vesperik’s long nights and harsh forested wilderness present a constant existential
threat to its inhabitants. Unity and order serve as the guiding light in the darkness
and most of its inhabitants have their roots in various military branches and
expeditionary forces. The Pyrex rules over a technocratic collective of guild-houses
who extend the influence of the grand city of Feulum into the untamed wilderness and
distant colonial provinces. The plane-spanning hive forest Beothran acts as a grand,
almost divine, but indifferent consciousness, spreading its influence throughout its
inhabitants, holding a grudge for each and every wound and scar the survivors dare
to inflict upon it.
Woodlands Survivor
The vast forest has been your home and prison. You are quick and quiet, for you know
what’s lurking in the dark.
Inspiration: Perception, Survival
Start with: Whisper 2
Borderlands Settler
You have carved out a bleak existence from amongst the thick black roots. Few would
survive the hardship you have endured.
Inspiration: Athletics, Willpower
Start with: Carry 2
Garrison Guard
You have dedicated yourself to the protection of humanity. You live to serve it until
your dying breath.
Inspiration: Brawling, Intuition
Start with: Authority 2
Scorchblade
You have pledged your existence to the bladed beacon. Brave the wilderness for a while
longer - the night will be over soon!
Inspiration: Endurance
Start with: Discipline 2, All tier 0 and 1 abilities from Duel.

Academa
You have learned about the world’s unending horrors from the comfort of the capital’s
walls. Guide your protectors into a better future.
Inspiration: Knowledge, Reason
Start with: Engineering 3 or Lore 3 or Medicine 3

Duiber Origins
While the evergreen plane might seem like a chilly paradise to outsiders, its
inhabitants often prove just how ruthless it can be. The mountain valleys have
produced countless isolated and strange cultures hostile to all who would intrude
upon them, and the Lords of the Valley rule over most of the plane’s interior with a
strict regime of occult collectivism. The Valley civilizations, “vallents”, heavily
subscribe to the idea of dualism, splitting both rule and society between the material
and spiritual roles. The coastfolk live bitter lives ravaged by flash-blizzards,
abandoned by their valley kin. Inside of the deep forests, the mystic followers of the
First Circle live in solitude, preparing for an unknown eternal ritual.
Coaster
The harsh eastern coasts bring naught but salt and cold wind, trapping you in
between the void and the realm of the Valley lords. Can you reclaim your existence
from these overwhelming forces?
Inspiration: Survival
Start with: Pilot 2, Carry 2
Vallentian
The valley’s splendor is unmatched by any other piece of the plane and it has allowed
you to enjoy a life of prosperity and calm in your tight-knit community. What could
have possibly led you to abandon it?
Inspiration: Intuition, Knowledge
Start with: Lore 2
Sectarian
You have both struggled and enjoyed your life in some far-removed forested glade. In
your habitat you are the perfect creature, yet a taste of the wider world is sure to clash
against your ingrained state of mind.
Inspiration: Stealth
Start with: Scout 2, Whisper 2

Circle Apostle
You have dedicated yourself to the true preservers of Halice. If anyone can mend this
broken world, it surely must be the wise circles of Duiber’s witches. You are sure that
no sacrifice could be too high to achieve this noble goal.
Inspiration: Perception
Start with: Lore 2, Medicine 2
Eye of The Lords
You have abandoned the simple life for the opportunity to serve the Lords of the Valley.
You are their eyes and claws, you are feared and respected, yet the idea that nobody
will mourn you still hasn’t left your mind.
Inspiration: Reasoning, Stealth
Start with: Authority 2

Lior Origins
Lioran deserts are soaked in the blood and sweat of those warring under the banners
of ancient clans and upstart warlords. The ancient buried cities tempt the desert’s
inhabitants with promises of old world technology and untold riches. Blood pacts are a
common custom and kin are often treated as mere currency for those unable to hold
their own. Slaver guilds scavenge the war-torn regions in search of able bodies to
excavate ancient relics. Vamor reigns supreme, enforcing ambition through blade and
bullet alike.
Tunnel Rat
You were taken by slaver companies as a child and have spent your formative years
digging through the perilous ruins in search of riches, and now freedom.
Inspiration: Dexterity, Survival
Start with: Interface 2
Clansman
You owe your life to your family and they owe their lives to you. This unbreakable
bond has been the only thing keeping you sane in these hostile deserts.
Inspiration: Intuition, Knowledge
Start with: Authority 2
Desert Skirmisher
Your life has been nothing but spilled blood and the smell of black powder. Pulling the
trigger comes to you as naturally as walking. You’ve been called heartless and cruel,
but it’s been a long time since that hatred got to you. Such are the simple ways of Lior.
Inspiration: Perception
Start with: Scout 2, All tier 0 and 1 abilities from Precision.
Reformationist
You see yourself and your city-dwelling kin as above the petty conflicts of the sands.
You have a grand vision for the future and no mercy for those who would impede it.
Inspiration: Reasoning, Willpower
Start with: Lore 2

Herd Nomad
War is the place where you and your kind are not. You’ve lived your life in the
company of the wind and the sand, refusing to partake in the widespread bloodshed.
Inspiration: Knowledge, Survival
Start with: Whisper 2
