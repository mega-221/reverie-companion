# Stalker

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 86-89 -->  
**Archetype:** Scoundrel · Traps · AreaControl  
**Recommended disciplines:** Shadow/Duel  

> The trees surrounding his perimeter all had malicious, highly toxic lichens growing on their bark. The paths leading to his shack had multiple concealed pits with falls landing onto carefully placed rusty, metal spikes. Various zones of his territory had an intricate tripwire system that would alert him if any kind of intruder dared to enter. He would spend the long nights maintaining this web of pain and watching the area from the dim rooms of his hideout, a weapon always at the ready. He had caught enough meat to last him a lifetime. In his domain, he was content. They left her alone to deal with the bounty she took on. No matter, she thought, they were never going to act as anything but bait to begin with. Luring the rebellious drake to the location was easy - she just needed some fresh carcasses. Trapping it within the cavern was an engineering problem, but ultimately one solvable through raw mechanical force. When all of this was finally done, all that was left was to immobilize its large clawed wings and finally engage it in a show of dominance. Any sane person would have feared for their life engaging a drake in combat under even the most favorable of circumstances. She, being the opposite, was the only person capable of singlehandedly dominating and retrieving a lost dragon. They had begun torching his woodland. For all of its faults and malice, he knew no other home, and he would not stand idly by as it was consumed. Gathering his few neighbours, they would embark on a campaign of retaliation. They’d work in tandem with the vile forest, arranging traps that would deliver their victims to its living roots and less human dwellers. When all was set and ready, he would join the hunt himself, abandoning what he had left in favor of the only place he ever called home. Despite Beothran’s horrors, he felt she would appreciate him more than any righteous easterner ever had. Stalkers are lurking hunters utilizing a wide arsenal of traps, hazards and capture equipment to displace and immobilize their targets. They are often loners living on the edges of civilisation, content with living in solitude while providing services that are often looked down upon in polite society. Some work as bounty hunters, others act as the protectors of nature, some become specialized operatives in more urban environments. In all cases, stalkers rely on their vast knowledge of their target area to spatially dominate their foes before using simpler tools and forces to complete their tasks.

## Class Features
#### Unfair Advantage — *Passive*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 0*
**Effect:** Whenever an enemy triggers one of your traps or hazards, choose one of the following: ● Heal 3 Strain. ● Gain a reaction. ● Gain +2 ACC against that enemy until the end of your next turn.

#### < Opportune Strike — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 1*
**0 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Triggers upon enemy triggering a trap or hazard. Gains +2 DMG if triggered by a trap.

#### > Morbid Showcase — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 2*
**2 SN** · +0 ACC · 1 DMG  
**Effect:** Inflict Push 1 in any direction.

## Path: Trapper
*Path that emphasizes traps over hazards and cares about setting up a perfect combo to knock out large opponents.*

#### Vicious Design — *Passive*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 2*
**Effect:** Unfair Advantage gains the following option when triggered by a trap: ● The enemy suffers Bleeding 2. ● The enemy suffers Feeble 3

#### > Reset Mechanism — *Utility*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Rearm a trap you have set up or set up a trap within your A.

#### < Merciful End — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 4*
**6 Strain** · +0 ACC · +6 DMG  
**Effect:** Triggers on an enemy reaching half or less of their maximum HP upon triggering your trap.

## Path: Poacher
*Path that specializes in area control and environmental hazards. Path Passive Ability:*

#### Cruel Domain — *Passive*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 2*
**Effect:** Successful attacks you perform on enemies standing within your hazards inflict Fractured equal to the amount of AP you spent on the attack.

#### < Better View — *Utility*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an enemy triggering your hazard. Gain and use a movement action.

#### | Hunting Grounds — *Utility*
*Requires: Stalker Rank 4*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Deploy a hazard.
