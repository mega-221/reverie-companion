# Wayfarer

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 98-101 -->  
**Archetype:** Guide · Navigator · Elusive  
**Recommended disciplines:** Protection  

> He ran through the grassy field, both arrows and snow raining down on him. Left, right, left right - the arrows swooped by him, yet none would hit their mark. No matter the conditions, he would deliver the message today, and neither a force of man nor nature would interrupt him. This unending source of resolve was inspired by neither love nor sentimentality, but was a product of a life that knew no other purpose and no other duty. He would collapse before the city gates, bloodied, utterly exhausted and more than anything - alive. This was just another night of exchanging stories beneath the stars, and he was leading just another group of traders through a famously dangerous passage. One of them had lost their bag earlier, another one was mauled by a wild animal, another yet had picked up a wicked fever. He gave up some of his own rations to help out, he was jumping at the chance to treat the bite wounds and he immediately knew which herbs could help out with the illness. Sitting around the campfire with them now, for the first time since maybe ever, he felt compelled to tell them of his world and his thoughts. He really tried to enjoy himself, yet something within him would not shut up about even this group’s fleeting existence. She was well known for her knowledge of the area and would often lead groups of travellers through the mountains, making sure to avoid the more popular roads and the highwaymen that stalked them. Her blade led from the front, her crossbow protected those in the back. Sometimes the wildlife would successfully wound her companions, sometimes the terrain would break a few of their bones, but nobody save herself had ever perished on one of her expeditions. Wayfarers are scouts and explorers who spend their time navigating the wilderness. While some work as messengers, others act as guardians and rangers of various regions, offering aid to those in need and defending their lands against would-be threats. They prefer lighter weapons and armour that allows them to swiftly traverse various rough terrain. Their excellent navigation skills set them apart as invaluable allies in dangerous areas.

## Class Features
#### Experienced Guide — *Passive*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 0*
**Effect:** Whenever your ally rolls an Endurance, Perception or Survival check, you may also roll it and add +2 to their roll if you succeed. Allies moving towards you gain +1 tile of Movement speed.

#### >> Target Call — *Utility Command*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 1*
**3 Strain**  
**Effect:** Target creature is Marked. Abilities performed by you and your allies targeting the marked creature gain +2 DMG and cost 2 Strain less. The mark disappears the next time you use this ability.

#### >> Point Out — *Defensive*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 2*
**0 Strain** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Give yourself or an ally within your M +4 EVA until your next turn. You heal 4 Strain for each attack that misses them or you during this period.

## Path: Vanguard
*Path that strives to keep its allies alive in battle through various defensive tactics.*

#### Defiant Example — *Passive*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever you suffer DMG, choose an ally to gain that much TVG. Interacting with an ally is now a free action.

#### < Savior — *Utility*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on ally getting damaged. If you can reach them with one movement action, you may move to them or you may gain +3 ACC on attacks targeting the creature that damaged them until the end of your next turn.

#### >> Vigil — *Defensive*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +4 EVA  
**Effect:** You and your allies within your M gain +1 Tile of movement speed until your next turn. If any of them would perform a death save, you may spend 6 Strain in order for it to automatically succeed.

## Path: Courier
*A path focused on survivability and elusiveness.*

#### Hard Target — *Passive*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever you move twice in a turn, gain +2 EVA until your next turn. You gain +4 Burden capacity.

#### > Rapid Traversal — *Utility*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain an additional Movement action. The next time you move this turn, gain +2 ACC and +2 DMG on your next attack if standing next to an enemy or +3 EVA if no enemies are within your A.

#### < Proximity Response — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Wayfarer Rank 4*
**3 SN** · +0 ACC · +1 DMG  
**Effect:** Triggers on entering or leaving of A of an enemy. Inflict Stun 2. The target cannot move until the end of their next turn (CL 16 Stance Save).
