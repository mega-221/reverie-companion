# Apothecary

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 102-105 -->  
**Archetype:** Scholar · Concoctions · Toolbox  
**Recommended disciplines:** Shadow/Duel  

> An old woman sat in a dimly lit room behind a desk laid with flasks and bottles. She ground some herbs in a mortar before finally mixing them into a thick, greenish liquid that has been placed over a light flame for hours. She would later use it to create an intense incense to ward off the creatures of the night. She would sometimes catch herself dreaming of the world beyond the forest, of what she could have seen out there, though the familiar mantra would quickly pop up - “You leave, they die.” He was by all means slower, weaker and less experienced than his fellows. Yet his strength laid in something else entirely - in his various belts and pouches and vials that were stored in them. A single cut would be enough for his venom to be applied, sending the enemy into manic fear before inflaming their airways so much as to completely close them. His comrades would sometimes call his methods vile and barbaric, but he had struggled to understand how they were any worse than hacking someone up into tiny pieces or violently breaking their bodily vessel. This way he at least got to avoid the fatigue such prolonged, intense combat could often carry. His position was overrun by the invading force, yet he still had a patient to finish treating. The enemy did not expect much resistance from the medical tent, allowing himself to recklessly stride into it, taunting the medic. He even laughed when the medic tackled him to the ground, a tiny scalpel in his hand. The laughter stopped when he recalled an earlier operation, his patient’s chest purple and swelling. He recalled the exact, precise cut of the scalpel he had made that saved the patient, and applied it ruthlessly to do the exact opposite. Hands shaking and mind spinning, he quickly got up and returned to his patient. He would later berate himself not for taking a life, but for failing to regain composure to save another. Apothecaries are scientists and doctors. Their curious minds are naturally drawn to the study of concoctions, poisons and anatomy. Some may spend their days endlessly tinkering with potions and ingredients, creating wildly potent brews, while others yearn to apply their knowledge in order to ensure the survival of their fellows. Others yet see the knowledge they possess for what it truly is - a weapon most powerful and unknown to the uneducated masses.

## Class Features
#### Alchemist’s Bag — *Passive*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 0*
**Effect:** You have access to a personal pouch of concoctions that don’t require ingredients to be prepared as long as you’re not trapped somewhere. Once per week, you may prepare 3 simple concoctions and 1 advanced concoction for that pouch. New concoctions take up the slots of existing ones.

#### >> Incision — *Utility*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Choose one: Harm: Perform an attack with +2 ACC that inflicts Bleeding 1. Any poisons applied to your weapon deal +2 DMG this attack. Heal: Perform a Defensive action and a CL 14 Survival check on yourself or an adjacent ally to reduce the target’s Bleeding by 2. If at half or less of their maximum VG, they restore 3 VG.

#### >> Theorem Application — *Defensive Action*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 2*
**0 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Consume or apply a concoction to yourself or an ally. If you are at half or more of your SL, heal 5 SN.

## Path: Venomdrop
*Poison-oriented path that aims to maximize their effects while conserving doses.*

#### Durable Fangs — *Passive*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 2*
**Effect:** Alchemist’s Bag gains a new slot for an advanced poison. Attacks you perform that exceed the target’s EVA score by 2 or more do not consume your weapon’s applied poison.

#### > Subject Observation — *Utility*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 3*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain +3 EVA against enemies who have been affected by your concoctions this turn until your next turn.

#### > Lethal Dose — *Utility*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 4*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** The target of your next attack with a poisoned weapon suffers the effects of that poison again on their next turn.

## Path: Surgeon
*Supportive path aimed at keeping allies alive and utilizing concoctions to increase their performance.*

#### Battlefield Medic — *Passive*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 2*
**Effect:** Alchemist’s Bag gains a new slot for an elixir. Utility actions that interact with allies are performed at Instant speed.

#### > Better Than New — *Utility*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** The next time you would apply a concoction to an ally or successfully perform a survival check on them this turn they gain +2 ACC on their next attack and heal 2 VG and 2 SN.

#### > Overdose — *Utility*
*Requires: Apothecary Rank 4*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Apply two doses of one or more concoctions to yourself or an ally.
