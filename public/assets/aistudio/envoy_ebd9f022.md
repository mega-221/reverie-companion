# Envoy

**Archetype:** Guide · Orders · Prediction  
**Recommended disciplines:** Leadership  

> As he was being transported to the scene they briefed him on the situation, on the trapped family and the maniac who was about to burn down the building they were in. Before entering his assistant passed him a tiny pistol which he tucked away in his pocket. Entering the building, he could see naught but dim, dusty hallways and narrow passages. He pleaded with the beast for nearly an hour before finally snapping at the gruesome plan the beast had presented concerning the little girl. He initiated the brawl with the intent to deceive, and when the beast got tired and pulled out a knife he placed a single precise shot in its dome. This was the last time he would agree to participate in such a negotiation. He knew they were after him for a long while. There was no easy way to purge the force of the traitors, so his best idea was to set a trap. He organized every detail of the ambush himself, he set his allies up in the exact positions and gave them a precise timeline. He had thought of everything, and all that was left was to convince his opponents he had not. He placed himself into an exposed office with a single entryway and waited. When they found him he could barely even strike at them before they knocked him to the ground, stabbed him in the back and shot his hands. His allies would reach them in time and capture them for memory extraction, yet his body would forever bear the scars of his master plan. She looked solemnly over the battlefield-to-become, despairing at the little hope they had left. They had lost too many men already, and the upcoming battle was about to become the final nail in their collective coffin. In what might have been an erratic and delusional idea, she donned her old armour and marched herself to the frontlines below. She had led them into death for too long, and if she was to do so again she could not bear witness. She would be the one to lead them into doom, and if luck allowed it, she would give them the tiniest of surges before fading into nothingness. As she made her way to the line of death, she had one last conversation with each of her close friends, those who had given her their all. Standing on the final rampart, she would prepare her rhetoric routine for the final time, producing a form-perfect rallying speech before descending into the mayhem. When she was impaled, she mustered everything she had to give them a final, frozen smile. Envoys are diplomats and commanders well versed in negotiation tactics and leadership roles. They prefer to attempt a conversation before resorting to more primitive means of conflict resolution. While some envoys prefer to lead from the front and amongst their allies, others work best within the confines of their own minds and the safety of the backlines, driving their allies to perform unimaginable feats through rhetoric and poetry.

## Class Features
#### Tactical Engine — *Passive*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 0*
**Effect:** You may use any order ability for only 1 AP. If you do, you may not use additional order abilities that turn and you suffer 1 Strain for each order target who fails to complete the order.

#### >>> Grand Plan — *Utility*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 1*
**4 Strain**  
**Effect:** Perform any order ability at no AP cost and choose up to 5 targets for it. During your next turn, you may perform another order ability at no AP cost targeting those same targets. You suffer 1 Strain for each target who fails to complete the order and heal 8 Strain if all targets complete both orders.

#### | Fallback Option — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 2*
**6 Strain** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This ability costs 3 Strain less if an ally failed to complete one of your orders since your previous turn.

## Path: Paragon
*Path that leads by example and reaps the full benefits of inspiring their allies.*

#### Centerpiece — *Passive*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 2*
**Effect:** You may freely target yourself with orders you perform in addition to their regular targets. You cannot suffer SN for not completing your own order and the first order you assign to yourself while using Grand Plan is always counted as completed. For each ally who completes an order after you have done so, both you and that ally restore 1 Strain.

#### >> Moment of Celebration — *Defensive*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 3*
**0 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Usable only if all targets of your order and you have completed that order since your previous turn. Restore 4 Strain and gain 1 temporary destiny point that lasts until the end of combat.

#### < Determined Execution — *Utility*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 4*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on your ability that would complete your order failing. You may reroll that ability.

## Path: Martinet
*Path that specializes in backline strategy, pushing their allies to their limits.*

#### Mastermind — *Passive*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 2*
**Effect:** Whenever all targets of an order complete it, restore 2 SN and gain a temporary destiny point that lasts until the end of the encounter. You may spend destiny points to give AP to the targets of your orders with a limit of 1 AP per target per combat round.

#### >> Field Promotion — *Utility*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Grant a willing ally 3 AP. They may use the same abilities twice during their turn and they suffer 4 SN.

#### < Disciplinary Action — *Utility*
*Requires: Envoy Rank 4*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an allies’ ability that would complete your order failing. They may increase their roll by 1 per 2 SN they spend.
