# Shadow

<!-- chapter: 06-disciplines | pages: 153-156 -->  
**Strike from the shadows, win by any means.**  
*Recommended for assassins, cutthroats and shady gadgeteers.*  

## Abilities

#### > Weakpoint Jab — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Stealth*
**0 Strain** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Inflict Pierce 1.

#### > Bump in The Dark — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Stealth*
**0 Strain** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +3 DMG and inflicts Pierce 2 against Unaware targets.

#### >> Hunker Down — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**0 Strain** · +1 EVA  
**Effect:** If no enemy targets you until your next turn, you enter Stealth.

#### > Ambush Shot — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 0 · Weapons: Stealth*
**0 Strain** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +3 ACC and +3 DMG against Unaware targets.

#### >> Lunge — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 1 · Weapons: Stealth*
**2 Strain** · -4 ACC · +3 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +4 ACC and inflicts Pierce 3 against Flanked and Unaware targets.

#### > Insidious Plot — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**2 Strain**  
**Effect:** Your next attack that uses Stealth weapons gains +2 ACC and +2 DMG

#### >> Moment of Reprieve — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**0 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Only usable while in Stealth. Restore 4 SN if your current SN is higher than half of your SL.

#### >> Opportunistic Angle — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**1 SN** · -3 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +3 ACC and inflicts Stun 2 against Flanked and Unaware targets.

#### >> Sacrificial Stab — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 2 · Weapons: Stealth*
**4 SN** · +4 ACC · +3 DMG  
**Effect:** You suffer -3 EVA against melee attacks until your next turn.

#### >> Lay to Rest — *1H Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**2 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +2 ACC and +10 DMG against Unaware targets. Only usable against targets who have entered combat this round.

#### < Familiar Tricks — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on you being afflicted by Stun. Reduce the level of that effect by 2.

#### > Entrenched Position — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Gain +2 EVA against ranged attacks from a target you attacked with a ranged attack this turn.

#### >> Daylight Robbery — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 3 · Weapons: Stealth*
**4 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Disarm the target and equip their weapon in your offhand if possible (CL 10 Stance save). The Stance save’s CL is 13 if the target is Flanked or 16 if the target is Unaware.

#### > Veil of Violence — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Enter Stealth if all enemies who targeted you since your previous turn are incapacitated or dead.

#### > Grim Satisfaction — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**0 SN**  
**Effect:** Restore 3 SN if you have killed or incapacitated an enemy while in Stealth this turn.

#### > Hidden Talent — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** The next attack you perform this turn gains +4 ACC if it uses a weapon type other than Stealth and if you haven’t used that weapon type before during this combat encounter.

#### >> Follow-up Execution — *1H Melee Attack*
*Requires: Class Rank 4 · Weapons: Stealth*
**6 SN** · -8 ACC · +10 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +8 ACC and inflicts Pierce 4 against targets that are Flanked and have been successfully attacked by your allies since your last turn.

#### < Murderous Frenzy — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Triggers on you incapacitating or killing an enemy. Gain 2 ACP. You may repeat abilities this turn.

#### < Gone and Forgotten — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Triggers on enemy melee attack targeting you missing. You gain an additional movement and immediately use it. If you get out of the line of sight of all enemies, you enter Stealth.

#### >> Feign Death — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**4 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Until your next turn, the next time you are targeted by an enemy attack you collapse, appear incapacitated to other enemies and enter Stealth. Your next attack against a Flanked or Unaware enemy during your next turn gains +5 ACC and +5 DMG.
