# Status Effects and Conditions

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 32-33 -->

Certain attacks and abilities can sometimes have unique or lasting effects on their
targets. Instead of repeating longer rules for these conditions, they are instead
explained here and given a keyword that actually appears in ability texts.
Pierce X - The attack inflicting this effect applies to bypasses up to X points of
armour on a successful hit.
Break X - The attack inflicting this effect depletes X points of armour on a
successful hit.
Stun X - Afflicted creature has -X on combat rolls. The level of the effect is reduced
by one at the end of the creature’s turn and by two upon performing a defensive
action.
Bleeding X - Afflicted creature loses X vigor at the beginning of its combat turn or
once every minute. The effect can be reduced by 1 by performing a defensive action
or by 2 by spending 2 AP to perform a successful survival skill check with a CL equal
to 12+X. Creatures wearing unbroken medium or heavy armour or using an
unbroken shield are immune to bleeding.
Burn X - Afflicted creature loses X vigor at the beginning of its turn. The level of
the effect is reduced by one when it damages the afflicted creature and by two
upon performing a defensive action. Burn deals +2 DMG to targets without armour.
Soulburn X - Afflicted creature loses X vigor at the beginning of its turn. The level
of the effect is reduced by two upon performing a defensive action. Upon reaching
0 VG, soulburn is cleansed and Burn 1 is applied.
X Temporary Vigor (TMV) - Afflicted creature has X additional vigor unaffected by
armour that is depleted before the main health pool.
Fractured X - Afflicted creature’s abilities cost +1 AP to perform. The level of the
effect is reduced by 1 at the end of the afflicted creature’s turn or by 2 upon
performing a defensive action and succeeding a CL 12+X discipline skill check.
Prone - Afflicted creature loses its reaction for this turn. At the start of its next
turn, it loses 2 AP and its movement action.
Feeble X - Afflicted creature deals -X DMG during its next turn.
Blind X - Afflicted creature suffers -8 ACC on all ranged attacks and abilities during
its next X turns.
Immobilized X - Afflicted creature is unable to move during its next X turns.
Unaware - Unaware creatures have 0 EVA.

Stealth - Other creatures are Unaware of the creature that is in Stealth. This effect
is lost upon entering a creature’s line of sight or performing an attack within their
line of sight.
Taunted - Afflicted creature has -2 on all attacks not targeting the creature that
inflicted taunt during its next 2 turns.
Intimidated - Afflicted creature has -2 on all attacks targeting the creature that
inflicted taunt during its next 2 turns.
Pull X - Moves the target X tiles towards you.
Push X - Moves the target X tiles away from you.
