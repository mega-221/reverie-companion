# Crisis

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 36-36 -->

Mentioned in the contexts of a character reaching their SL and defying convictions,
a crisis is the mental breaking point that occurs as a consequence of intense
psychological stress.
When faced with a crisis, the character must resolve it in one of multiple ways:
● Lower the rank of the chosen/violated conviction
● Manifest/increase the rank of the failing for that conviction
● Spend your destiny point maximum
● Abandon an aspect
