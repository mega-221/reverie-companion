# Combat Encounters

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 23-23 -->

Combat encounters are more rigid than other parts of gameplay as they aim to
more rigidly simulate the events of the scene. In them, characters use their
equipment and abilities in tactical gameplay in order to incapacitate their enemies
or achieve another goal. They feature a defined timeline and turn order as well as
rules for how much time actions take to perform. Combat encounters involving
more than a couple of characters should be played out on a square grid in order to
make them simpler to follow.
Turn Order
Who goes first in an encounter should be determined by simple reasoning or
agreed upon by the players if they are going first. You could say the character with
the highest REF would go first, or you could say it would be the character who
threw the first punch in a brawl, or anything else that makes sense for that
situation.
After the first character finishes their turn, a character from the opposing side of
the conflict, your enemy, is given a turn in the same way as determined by the
Narrator. The combat round consists of a constant exchange of allied and enemy
character turns until all present characters have played out their turns. If there are
more enemies than allies or vice versa, the side with more characters gets to play
out their remaining turns in sequence after the side with less characters is fully
finished for the round.
Rounds and Turns
Combat in Reverie happens in rounds where each combatant gets a turn to
perform their actions. Take note of this distinction as it is important for some
effects and abilities.
Your Options
During a single combat round, each player characters and most humanoid
non-player characters has:
● 1 Movement Action to use during their turn
● 3 Action Points (ACP) to spend on abilities during their turn
● 1 Reaction that can be used at any point in the round
