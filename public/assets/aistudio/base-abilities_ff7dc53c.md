Abilities
Most actions you can perform in combat in Reverie are abilities. They have
specified costs, requirements and effects. You will learn more about specific
abilities in the sections regarding different disciplines - collections of abilities that
encompass different styles of combat. The amount of ACP an ability costs is
dependant on its speed, which can be either of the following:
[ >>> ] Slow (3 action points)
[ >> ] Average (2 action points)
[ > ] Swift (1 action point)
[ I ] Instant (0 action points)
[ < ] Reaction (1 reaction)
Stronger and more impactful abilities tend to require more ACP as well as more
strain.

While different characters may have varying abilities depending on their class,
disciplines or equipment, all characters always have access to this set of 7 basic
