# Melee Combat

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 29-29 -->

When engaged in melee combat, to perform attacks combatants must be within the
effective range of their weapons which is Adjacent by default but can be modified
through the use of abilities.
Flanking
If a character is engaged in melee combat with multiple opponents all of their
enemies gain +1 ACC on attacks targeting the flanked character per enemy engaged
beyond the first. The maximum value this bonus can reach is +4. For example, two
bandits fighting against a player character would each receive +1 ACC to their rolls.
Using Both Hands
If a character’s weapon doesn’t take up two hands already, they gain the ability to
pick up an offhand to complement their main weapon.
Any one-handed melee weapon can be used as an offhand, providing +1 EVA to the
character using it and allowing them to also perform abilities that would require it.
Shields
Shields, with exceptions, focus on predictable defense, offering an additional way to
gain AP. They are an excellent choice for a sidearm if you require reliable
survivability.
Other Options
There also exists a wide variety of tools and combat items that can come in handy
in a combat encounter like pistols, hooks, bombs and so on. Keep in mind that you
might also sometimes want a free hand in order to interact with the environment.
