# Classes, Disciplines & Aspects Overview

Classes Overview
Classes are character archetypes that exist to guide players into developing their
characters. They are often centred around a specific mechanic or type of
equipment, providing a core goal for progression while also laying out potential
directions to branch off into. Classes are your core, but they do not limit you in any
way besides providing you with some unique abilities. Your class also ranks up
passively with your tier, starting at rank 0 in tier 1 and increasing by 1 each time
your tier does up to a maximum of rank 4. This will give you access to new class
features and may be relevant for some abilities.
Class Feature
Class Feature consists of one or multiple passive and active abilities that make it
unique. They may be available right away or unlocked upon reaching a higher Class
rank.
Paths
Once a character reaches Tier 3, they gain the option to settle into one of multiple
available “subclasses” - paths that their character can do down. They differ in
available abilities and serve as both an important mechanical and roleplay decision.
Paths serve as a continuation of class progression, their abilities being unlocked the
same way as class abilities. In order to adopt a specific path, it may be required for
the character to do something in the game to reach the point where that path
seems natural to follow.
Choosing a Class
If you are unsure which class would be best suited for your new character or you
simply want a summary of all classes, refer to the class archetype list in chapter 5.
They represent thematic groupings of classes that should hopefully give you the
idea of what kind of characters would be better suited for which classes.

Disciplines Overview
Disciplines are collections of abilities gathered around a singular theme or style of
combat. Once chosen through advancements, their abilities can be picked up at any
time through advancements or by other means like training or studying. When a
new discipline is chosen, the character may learn up to two of its rank 0 abilities for
free. Disciplines are there to provide players with a flexible character progression
system that lets them create unique builds within classes for their characters,
allowing them a great degree of customization. Every character gains a choice of a
single discipline at creation. Disciplines are covered in depth in chapter 6.
Aspects Overview
Aspects are thoughts and internalized behaviors of characters which can alter the
ways they interact with the environment, mostly in out of combat scenarios. They
may provide alternative ways to deal with specific problems, adjust the costs of
performing specific actions or present ways to gamble on possible outcomes of
events. They are a modular way to make characters unique and provide niche
gameplay alterations that will further specialize them. Aspects are covered in depth
in chapter 7.
