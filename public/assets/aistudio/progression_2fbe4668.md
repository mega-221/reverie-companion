# Progression and Advancements

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 53-53 -->

Progression
Characters in Reverie have multiple improvement tracks with varying speeds:
Experience Points
Experience points (XP) are the main metric of progression, serving as a currency to
improve core and special skills of a character. XP does not have a set rate at which
it should be earned, rather the rate should be set by the Narrator for the intended
campaign length. Gaining XP also eventually leads to level increases and then to tier
increases.
Leveling Up
Upon reaching a new level characters gain the ability to pick out two
advancements from the list of available advancements for their current or any
lower tier. In addition, reaching a new tier gives the characters additional stat
increases defined for each tier. Each new tier gives the character +1 VL, +1 SL, +1
attribute point and +1 DP maximum. Tiers beyond tier 5 only give +1 VL and +1 SL.
Advancements
Advancements are additional upgrades gained through levels with each new level
allowing 2 advancements to be chosen from the current and all previous character
tiers. All tiers have at least these available advancements to choose from:
[x1] +1 ACC
[x1] +1 EVA
[x2] +2 VL
[x2] +2 SL
[x4] Learn new discipline ability
Other advancements are available in specific tiers only:
[x1] Unlock a new aspect slot - Tiers 1-5
[x1] Learn a new discipline - Tiers 1-4
[x1] Increase a conviction to rank 5 - Tier 4
[x1] Increase a skill to rank 5 - Tier 5
