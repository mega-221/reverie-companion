# Levels and Tiers

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 51-52 -->

Levels
Levels are a representation of life experience of a character, increasing by 1 for
every 5 points of experience the character has. Unlike in many other TTRPGs, in
Reverie it is rarely recommended that you start out at level 1. Levels in this game
scale linearly and starting at a very low level will restrict your progression by a
decent amount until you reach higher levels. While this is a good representation of
common people, such individuals can scarcely survive the more dangerous parts of
Dunyor. Keep in mind that levels are not arbitrary power ups but a timeline of
growth.
Tiers
Tiers are broad groupings of levels that describe the general amount of experience
a character has. They are useful when it comes to quickly determining the power
level of creatures and characters as well as solid guidelines for start and end points
of a campaign. The tiers are as following:
Tier 1 (levels 1-4) - Nobody
Attribute Rank Limit: 2
Skill Rank Limit: 2
This tier represents the faceless masses. The world will not be kind to you.
Tier 2 (levels 5-8) - Promising Soul
Attribute Rank Limit: 2
Skill Rank Limit: 3
This tier is the earliest recommended starting point for a character. These
characters are a great starting point for longer campaigns or more horror-oriented
narratives, being mostly average with a few specific areas where they can shine.
Tier 3 (levels 9-12) - Competent Individual
Attribute Rank Limit: 3
Skill Rank Limit: 3
This tier is the best starting point for starting in the middle of action. Tier 3
characters are still frail but they have begun to access tools that can turn the tides
in their favour.

Tier 4 (levels 13-16) - Skilled Professional
Attribute Rank Limit: 3
Skill Rank Limit: 4
This tier is best used for one-shots and shorter adventures. Characters are
generally competent in most areas and a balanced party can really go a long way.
These characters will rarely just fail because they are incompetent and will instead
begin to fail when they cannot bring themselves to sacrifice an important part of
themselves.
Tier 5 (levels 17-20) - Renowned Veteran
Attribute Rank Limit: 4
Skill Rank Limit: 4
This tier represents a significant milestone for lesser characters. Tier 5 characters
are well rounded and often exceptional individuals in their chosen areas of
development.
Tier 6 (levels 21-24) - Legendary Hero
This is the first endgame tier. These characters are world class in what they do and
their presence is fitting in the highest stakes adventures. They tend to either be
known far and wide or so secretive as to not be recognized by anyone but a select
few individuals.
Is there more?
You could theoretically go up in levels and tiers as much as you wanted. These later
tiers are not defined as their progression structure is identical. It is generally not
recommended to go that high in levels as characters tend to fill out all of their
weaknesses and become too well-rounded to be interesting. Tier 6 characters
should have already reached the peak of what is possible and done everything there
was to do for their world. Tier 7 characters should be dead.
