# Arbiter

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 114-117 -->  
**Archetype:** Mystic · Gravity · Augury  
**Recommended disciplines:** Zeal, Mysticism  
**Recommended special skills:** Deception, Lore, Carry, Whisper  

> A man sits under the cloudy, silvery sky, his body still yet his eyes twitching wildly. In his thoughts, he is far above the valley, soaring through the cold heavens above. His wings click and shift, producing crisp, metallic sounds. He knows the storm is coming, so he flies southward to warn his kin. Landing on a predetermined signal pad, his fellow takes note, and the man disassociates from the avian construct. He breathes in a few times to acclimate to his surroundings before getting up and walking back into his home. They send her in only when they’re absolutely sure of the suspect’s identity and absolutely sure that they would be in danger to even attempt to confront them. She is silent like a flap of feathers in the night, her dark clothing blends into the shadows of buildings and trees alike. She cares not to make a scene, but to extract a swift confession and deliver a verdict should it be required. Most of them fold when she lifts their fragile body up to the ceiling or high in the air. Those who do not usually fold when she bends one of their limbs to encourage honesty. The remaining ones break completely when she is unsatisfied with their testimonies. Children, women and men alike hide from her when she walks down the street like she is some sort of boogeyman, yet she has learned not to pay them any mind. They’ve thought her a cold monster before she became cruel, so why should she deprive them of their fantasy? The creatures of the forest were rapidly descending upon the village, and most of its defenders had already been torn apart. It was never his job to eliminate threats, but to buy time either through repelling them or through acting as a bulwark to those who could not fight. This noble goal had been the thing that had kept him sane and focused for all these years, allowing him to claw back countless souls from the woodland’s malignant branches. Duty and life, one in the same to his peaceful mind, to abandon one would be to abandon both. He would call each and every crow in sight, arranging them in a cryptic flight pattern above the crumbling town. The creatures would begin to struggle with even the most basic movements under the omen and at the mercy of the flying, bloodthirsty knives swirling about. Once he was sure everyone had long departed, he would calm the storm and sit down. He would look at the dark sky one last time before breathing in, never to let go. Arbiters are the mystics of Trumst proficient in the control of gravitational forces and control of the omnipresent crow constructs of Dunyor. They serve the forces of reason and order, acting as surveyors and surveillance specialists, and if need be, potent group disruptors capable of misaligning the limbs of enemy combatants in precisely the right time at precisely the wrong place.

## Class Features
#### Eye of the Murder — *Passive*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 0*
**Effect:** At the beginning of your turn, you call a crow construct to your vicinity. Whenever you perform an attack, you may send out two crows to: Fracture: Inflict Fractured 1 on the target. Crows leave your vicinity upon being used by this ability.

#### > Unseen Aid — *Utility*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 1*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Call a nearby crow construct to your vicinity (that stays) to perform your choice of the following: Assist: Gain 1 AP that persists between rounds and does not count towards your default cap of 3 AP. Pinpoint: Move your MO. Shelter: Prevent the next instance of DMG you would receive and suffer twice as much SN instead.

#### >> Forcefield — *Defensive*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 2*
**3 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Inflict Push 1 on all enemies standing in your A and all enemies who enter your A until the next turn and heal 1 Strain per inflicted Push. Enemies may only be affected by the second condition of this ability once per turn.

## Path: Inquisitor
*Path that fully prioritizes spatial control and mechanical force to suppress any obstacles it encounters.*

#### Subjugating Presence — *Passive*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 2*
**Effect:** Status effects you inflict upon Intimidated enemies are increased by +1. Eye of The Murder gains the following option: Terrorize: inflict Intimidated which lasts until the target successfully attacks you instead of its usual duration.

#### >> Retribution — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 3*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst If the target attacked during their previous turn, inflict Feeble 2. If the target used a defensive ability during their previous turn, inflict Bleeding 2. If the target used a utility ability, inflict Stun 2.

#### | Grand Authority — *Utility*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 4*
**8 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Gain 2 AP. You may spend destiny points to pay for this ability’s Strain cost.

## Path: Augur
*Path that calls upon the countless Observer constructs to do its bidding and surveil large areas.*

#### Planar Attunement — *Passive*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 2*
**Effect:** You may dismiss crows to pay for 2 SN per crow when performing abilities. Eye of The Murder gains the following option: Omen: Arrange the crows in a pattern above the target, inflicting Immobilize 1 and Stun 1.

#### < Anomalous Findings — *Utility*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 3*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Triggers on an enemy performing an ability that costs 4+ Strain. Summon a crow construct to your vicinity.

#### >>> Featherstorm — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Arbiter Rank 4*
**3 SN** · +2 ACC · +3 DMG  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Perform this attack on a number of targets equal to the number of crows in your vicinity each turn. Casting and upkeeping this ability costs 1 Destiny Point. If this cost is not paid, you suffer 3 Failing levels or 20 DMG instead.
