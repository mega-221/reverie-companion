# Exert

<!-- chapter: 02-playing-the-game | pages: 18-18 -->

After performing any kind of roll, a player unsatisfied with the result may attempt
to push their character beyond their regular limits, exerting the roll.
In doing so, the character will suffer strain equal to 25% of their strain limit,
rounded up. The player may choose any die from the exerted roll and reroll it.
Exerting is not limited by anything but the character’s capacity for strain and can
be repeated as many times as wanted as long as the excess strain doesn’t kill the
character.
