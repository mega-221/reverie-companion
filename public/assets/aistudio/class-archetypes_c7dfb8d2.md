CH V. Classes
This chapter covers all available class options as well as what exactly they provide in
terms of gameplay. All classes are introduced through three in universe
perspectives which showcase what a character of this class might grapple with
throughout a campaign. After that, the general gameplay loop is explained and all of
the class’ feature abilities are listed alongside recommendations (which do not have
to be followed) for a conventional build.
Class Archetypes
In order to further help you pick out a class, this archetype list should hopefully
illustrate the broad strokes of each of them. Feel free to skip over classes that do
not interest you here while delving deeper into those that intrigue you. Also keep in
mind that the class is not the all-defining feature of your character and that
disciplines and aspects can greatly warp how your character interacts with the
world. Characters with the same class could end up vastly different depending on
those other choices, so you should not feel the pressure to strictly adhere to
whatever idea a class presents you with. Experimentation with niche options and
tactics is not just supported, but encouraged.
The full list of class archetypes and classes is:
Champion - Physically capable warriors who put a great deal of effort into
bettering their bodies.
● Berserker - Brutal melee warriors
● Legionary - Disciplined soldiers and combat engineers
● Warden - Heavily armored guardians and diplomats
● Marksman - Precise hunters and snipers
Scoundrel - Shady and resourceful operatives who prefer subversive tactics and
unfair advantages.
● Duelist - Cunning fighters and socialites
● Rogue - Stealthy thieves and operatives
● Stalker - Crafty lurkers and trappers
● Gunslinger - Firearms wielding vigilantes and mercenaries

Guide - Charismatic and inspiring leaders who focus on bolstering their allies and
providing support.
● Bard - Ambedo-powered bannermen
● Envoy - Charismatic leaders and tacticians
● Wayfarer - Resilient navigators and couriers
Scholar - Educated specialists and ambedo experts who prefer to rely on science
and technology over more primitive approaches.
● Apothecary - Intelligent alchemists and poisoners
● Clairvoyant - Ambedo scientists and detectives
● Mechanist - Ambedic drone and mech operators
Mystic - Radical idealists who manifest their Season’s will through their strict
adherence to its ideals.
● Arbiter - Gravity bending mages of Trumst
● Empath - Cryptic mind mages of Bruma
● Sorcerer - Passionate pyromancers of Vamor
● Witch - Occult necromancers of Halice
Aberration - Beastly individuals possessing terrible abilities and bearing
dehumanizing curses.
● Protean - Void-touched shapeshifters
● Vessel - Parasite-infested blood mages
