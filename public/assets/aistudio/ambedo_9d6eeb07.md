# Ambedo

A cyan-blue matter known as ambedo populates the majority of Dunyor’s surface in
various concentrations. While some places have only faint hints of its particles, in
places with higher concentrations ambedo dust can form into ambedic crystals –
empty vessels capable of imprinting and storing human emotion and thus memory.
The study and application of this phenomena, known as ambedics, is in an ongoing
and scattered infancy, relegated to sponsored scholars and daring explorers of the
planar edges.
The simplest use of the ambedic crystals is as storage of memories. When a
sentient being experiences a particularly strong emotion near an empty crystal,
their current memories and point of view get imprinted onto the crystal and may
later be accessed in full immersion by anyone in possession of an eidoloscope, a
specialized memory delving device often employed by clairvoyants.
Alternatively, some ambedic crystals are found already filled with something
incomprehensible to an eidoloscope. These are ambedic batteries, energy vessels
with a seemingly infinite lifespan yet a strict capacity limit. Going over the limit
shatters the crystal, making it vital not to accidentally overwork whichever system
was utilizing it. Single batteries are used to power simple mechanisms or construct
drones, while multiple may be incorporated into larger machinery. These batteries
are exceedingly rare, and nobody in the public eye seems to be aware of how they
were created.
Empty crystals may also be turned into short distance wireless communication
devices via etching, enabling them to be used as relays between each other and
sentient beings. This technology powers all kinds of sophisticated prosthetics and
serves as the primary means of controlling constructs.
Finally, any ambedic crystal can simply be crushed into dust and applied as a kind of
psychological weapon. Inhaling or ingesting ambedic dust causes most humans to
enter madness, wild mood swings or a vegetative trance. Prolonged exposure may
make these effects permanent.
