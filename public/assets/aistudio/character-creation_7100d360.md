# Character Creation

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 55-55 -->

Having an overview of all the elements of a character, we can finally discuss how to
actually make one. The full process in its entirety, once you know which level you
are starting at, involves in order:
● Coming up with the basics like the name and character concept
● Choosing an origin
● Picking possible special skills
● Assigning your attribute and skill points
● Picking a class
● Spending advancements
● Picking your discipline(s) and the abilities gained from them
● Picking your convictions
● Picking your aspect(s) if you want to
● Discussing stating equipment with your Narrator
