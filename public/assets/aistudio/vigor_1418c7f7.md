# Vigor

<!-- chapter: 03-survival-and-combat | pages: 19-20 -->

Vigor (VG) is a measure of a character’s resistance to harm and health. It is lost
when the character receives damage (DMG), be it in combat or otherwise. Every
character starts with a vigor limit (VL) of 5 which increases by 1 each time they
reach a new Tier. VL can be further increased by picking the relevant
Advancements when leveling up. Narratively, vigor serves to represent minor
injuries that wouldn’t significantly impact the combatant’s performance.
Reaching 0 VG
The point at which a character reaches 0 VG is the first time they get properly
injured in the encounter. They finally got too tired to properly dodge or parry the
enemy’s attacks, and something hit them hard. Adrenaline is flooding their system
so they aren’t exactly sure what happened yet, but they know they must make a
choice:
● Manifest Will
● Accept Fate
● Defy Death
Manifesting your will will allow your character to perform one final action which
will automatically and critically succeed before they die. This should not be chosen
lightly.

Accepting your fate will lead to the character falling unconscious and suffering an
injury. They will wake up if a successful CL 14 survival check is performed or after
sufficiently resting. If their wounds are too severe, however, they will require
further medical aid in order to avoid death.
Defying death is a compromise in which the character exposes themselves to
further danger, risking it all, in order to complete their goal. From that point
onwards until they fall unconscious, each instance of DMG they receive can become
lethal - whenever they are damaged they roll a single d6. No skills or attributes
affect this roll. Should the result be 4 or higher, the character will tank the blow and
continue to live. Otherwise, they will be immediately struck down.
When the encounter ends, the character still sustains an injury, falls unconscious
and must then be stabilized via a survival or medicine check in order to avoid
further injuries and death.
Regaining VG
HP can be regained through multiple means. The most common is to perform a
rest, but it can also be achieved through some consumables and even some
abilities.
Enemy VG
To further elaborate on the nature of VG, we should explain how enemy VG should
be interpreted. Just like your own, their VG is a measure of how much damage they
can take without taking a hit to combat performance. Reducing their VG to 0 opens
them up for you to exact your will upon them, be it by inflicting a debilitating injury
or executing them on the spot. Keep in mind that some determined or powerful
enemies might have abilities that allow them to evade such a fate in a similar way to
player characters.
