# Core Skills

<!-- chapter: 02-playing-the-game | pages: 15-15 -->

Each character has access to a total of 12 unique core skills, 3 nested in each of the
attributes. They have a total of 8 ranks which each represent +1 added to the result
when used to perform a roll. These skills represent the learned capability to
perform specific actions within the world.
To increase the rank of a skill the character has to spend 1/1/2/2/3/3/4/4
experience points. Skills may be increased at any point during downtime as long as
the character has enough xp. The full list of core skills includes:
GRT:
Athletics - Raw physicality. Your ability to influence the world with your body’s
power.
Brawling - Hand-to-hand combat. Your unarmed combat prowess and the ability to
intimidate others.
Endurance - Bodily resilience. Your ability to withstand pain and various harsh
conditions.
REF:
Dexterity - Balance and coordination. Your ability to perform elegant and acrobatic
movements.
Perception - The senses. Your ability to detect things in your surroundings.
Stealth - Elusiveness. Your ability to hide from others and pass unnoticed.
INT:
Knowledge - The encyclopedia of facts. Your ability to recall basic information
about the world.
Reasoning - Critical thinking. Your ability to apply logic and notice patterns.
Survival - Bodily familiarity. Your ability to perform first aid and take care of your
needs.
PSY:
Intuition - Empathy. Your ability to sense the motivations of others.
Persuasion - Charm. your ability to negotiate and barter.
Willpower - Mental resilience. Your ability to resist the will of others.
