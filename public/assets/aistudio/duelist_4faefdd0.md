# Duelist

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 74-77 -->  
**Archetype:** Scoundrel · Elegant · Tempo  
**Recommended disciplines:** Duel  
**Recommended special skills:** Stance, Deception, Authority, Lore  

> As the feast was nearing its end, she arose from her seat, an old flimsy rapier on her hip, and issued a challenge for the rank to her commanding officer. He snickered among his close associates, calling on his armored warden to duel in his stead. She engaged the iron golem in a mockery of a fencing match, making provocative yet harmless strikes at his iron shell. When the golem decided to end the duel by simply coming at her, she made a final feint, snapped open his visor and shot him with a gun concealed within her sleeve. While she may have been shaken, her body would never betray her by showing it to the silent crowd. He had been sent to quell a peasant uprising in the borderlands. His trained men had made quick work of the rabble. Descending upon the rebels’ last stronghold, they would be greeted not by desperate resistance, but a single, disheveled man, offering to resolve the issue with only a single death. They agreed on the terms - immediate surrender or mercy for the rebels. His shiny engraved saber had crossed with many blades, yet never had it met a piece of iron as crude and frail as his opponent’s rusty sword. In but a few swings he had cut off the rebel leader’s hand, yet the sight of his surrendered body kneeling in the mud stopped his arm from performing the final swing. Without a single word, he would turn back and order his men to depart the hamlet. When she woke up her rival’s mercenaries had already begun torching the manor. She would usher her children and servants through the secret passage, shoving various heirlooms into their shaking hands. By the time they were all safe her muscles were tired and her throat burned. Coughing, half dressed and a molten blade in hand, she had emerged through the front entrance and waved a challenge to her murderers. They had to see her go down if her children were to ever live in peace. Her broken, charred body would display all of her life’s achievements to a dozen of them before it finally ran out of adrenaline. In her final moments, she laid down her blade and kneeled before her decapitators, taking one final look at the stars. Duelists are skilled and elegant fighters who specialize in single combat. Unlike the more savage warriors of the world, they are more akin to entertainers, putting on a good show in addition to slaying their foe. They prefer one handed weapons paired with either a parrying tool or a sidearm, utilizing a complex combat technique in order to gain advantage. Their form is perfect and their reflexes sharp - no trick will surprise them.

## Class Features
#### Duel Etiquette — *Passive*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 0*
**Effect:** After performing an attack that requires 2 or more AP, enter the Offensive Stance. After using a defensive action enter the Defensive Stance. Offensive Stance: You gain +1 DMG and your melee attacks inflict Pierce 1. Defensive Stance: You gain +1 EVA.

#### >> Fleche — *Melee Attack*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 1*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +3 ACC if you have moved towards your target this turn. Offensive Stance: Gain a free movement action. This attack deals +2 DMG.

#### >> Anticipate — *Defensive*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 2*
**2 SN** · +1 EVA  
**Effect:** Offensive Stance: During your next turn gain +2 ACC for each attack made against you from now until that turn. Defensive Stance: Heal 3 Strain for each attack made against you until your next turn.

## Path: Nemesis
*A risky offensive path that sacrifices all defenses for unparalleled lethality.*

#### Rising Momentum — *Passive*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 2*
**Effect:** Gain +1 DMG on each consecutive successful attack. When you are hit while this bonus is active, that attack gains the bonus instead and the bonus is reset.

#### > Lethal Tempo — *Utility*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 3*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Whenever you deal DMG this round, heal an equal amount of Strain.

#### >> Blade Waltz — *Melee Attack*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 4*
**3 SN** · +2 ACC · +1 DMG  
**Effect:** Offensive Stance: This attack may be repeated for any number of different enemies. Movement actions may be used in between these additional attacks.

## Path: Gallant
*Balanced path that prioritizes defense while waiting for a perfect moment to strike.*

#### Rising Tension — *Passive*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 2*
**Effect:** Gain an additional reaction every round while in Defensive Stance. Fleche has +1 DMG and inflicts Pierce 2 in the Defensive Stance.

#### > Backup Plan — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 3*
**3 SN** · +2 ACC · +1 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack doesn’t suffer penalties for being performed at A range. Only usable with a 1H ranged weapon. Enter any Stance or remove your current Stance.

#### >> Coup — *Defensive*
*Requires: Duelist Rank 4*
**6 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Disarm an adjacent target (CL 18 Stance Save).
