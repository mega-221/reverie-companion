# Legionary

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 62-65 -->  
**Archetype:** Champion · Breacher · Formation  
**Recommended disciplines:** Warfare/Duel, Leadership  
**Recommended special skills:** Carry, Engineering, Stance, Scout  

> He was not just another cog in the machine. His will and his might would change this broken world. Equipped with the latest and best equipment Eterion had to offer, he would tame the unreality with his virtuous comrades. His blade had been sharpened for hours. His armour shone like a bright, silvery gemstone. His shield concealed a tiny cannon, the pride and joy of his homeland’s military engineers. All of his bravado would fade at the sight of his own blood soaking an enemy’s crude, serrated implement. She had spent enough time in this place to understand its calculus. The ore had to reach the port in two days, passing through a famously lethal mountain pass, then through an active warzone only to end up in cannibal infested woodlands. The price of ore is well known, as is the price of a soul. She added up the costs and assigned the indebted men and women to their duties, those more experienced to the transport, those less fortunate to “protection” squads. She thought this exchange rational, thinking she would feel at peace with her own duty when it was time for her to do the same. They stood shoulder to shoulder, inseparable since childhood. They had the entire song and dance down to a science. A shieldbearer in the front, a gunman to take out those who would sabotage their little formation in the back and two warriors to the sides, mowing down whatever got caught on their long blades. A moment of distraction was all it took for the firebomb to hit them, for the gunman to be set ablaze. With all of the grace and delirium of an amputee, they would march on like usual, holding on to their crushed souls, as if that would bring him back from the ashes. Legionaries are trained and versatile servants. They perform a variety of duties ranging from warfare to construction and logistics, serving as the backbone of any modern task force. Some specialize in close quarters combat, others prefer ranged weapons, what binds them are the creative ways in which they outplay their opponents. No matter their origin or style, legionaries are industrious combat engineers disciplined enough to achieve any task, given enough resources or souls.

## Class Features
#### Always Prepared — *Passive*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 0*
**Effect:** Heal 2 Strain whenever you use a consumable item or perform an attack with a weapon you have equipped since the beginning of your previous turn. Suffer 6 Strain whenever you witness an ally being reduced to 0 HP.

#### >> Tactical Breach — *Utility*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 1*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** You may freely holster any weapons and items and suffer -4 EVA until your next turn. Perform a +2 ACC attack using a newly equipped weapon or consumable that heals 2 Strain of all allies with half or more of their SL if successful.

#### >> Solid Formation — *Defensive*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 2*
**0 SN** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** You and all allies that are in your A or are also adjacent to these allies are considered a part of your formation and cannot be flanked. Until your next turn allies in your formation gain +1 EVA and you suffer 1 Strain whenever they are damaged.

## Path: Exemplar
*Path that specializes in decisive strikes and capitalizes on any opportunities that present themselves.*

#### Perfect Moment — *Passive*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 2*
**Effect:** Each time an enemy fails to hit you or an ally within your M you gain one Perfect Moment Charge. Upon reaching the maximum of 4 Charges, you may spend them while performing Tactical Breach in order to empower it while suffering an additional -2 EVA: Bold Offensive: You and an ally of your choice gain a Temporary Destiny Point that lasts until the end of the encounter. Aligned Axis: You and an ally of your choice gain +1 AP and +2 ACC until your next turn. ●

#### >> Measured Response — *Melee/Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 3*
**4 SN** · +3 ACC · -3 DMG  
**Effect:** When you perform this attack, any number of your allies may also perform it at no Strain cost. Everyone who manages to hit their target gains +3 EVA until their next turn. ●

#### >> Secure Position — *Defensive*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 4*
**0 SN** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** If you have successfully performed a Tactical Breach during your previous turn, this ability costs 1 AP less to perform. Restore 4 Strain up to half of your SL maximum.

## Path: Banneret
*Path that focuses on ally support and coordinating formations.*

#### Bound Fates — *Passive*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 2*
**Effect:** You and all allies that are in your C or are also adjacent to these allies are considered a part of your formation. Abilities affecting your allies cost 1 Strain less to perform while you are in a formation with two or more allies.

#### >> Triumphant Presence — *Defensive*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 3*
**4 Strain** · +2 EVA  
**Effect:** This ability additionally costs 1 Destiny Point if it has been used the previous turn. Allies within your formation gain your choice of the following until your next turn: ● +2 ACC on ranged attacks, -2 EVA ● +2 TVG, -2 tiles of movement ● +1 tile of range on melee attacks, +1 Strain cost on abilities.

#### < Hopebearer — *Utility*
*Requires: Legionary Rank 4*
**5 Strain**  
**Effect:** Whenever an attack would reduce you to 0 or less HP, an ally within your C may take that much damage +3 instead of you.
