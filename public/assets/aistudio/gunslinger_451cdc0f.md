# Gunslinger

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 78-81 -->  
**Archetype:** Scoundrel · RapidFire · Explosive  
**Recommended disciplines:** Precision  

> He could not save his mother from the marauders. He spent hours wandering the wasteland, aimlessly looking for his final resting place. He finally sat down on the dusty, dry ground in the shade of some tall rocks, his look gazing at his final sunset. He must have sat there for over an hour, shifting his look from the sun to his father’s pistol. It did not save his mother, but it did save him, and that must count for something. His life was already gone, what difference did it make if he ended it all here or in some desperate attempt to redeem his failure while saving another life? As he dwelled on these thoughts, he felt a thirst return to his throat, and his stomach alerted him that he would in fact, not mind a dinner. She was known amongst her colleagues for rarely using her gun. Despite this, she practiced her aim more than anyone. Her work in the Etergarde had increasingly been forcing her into hostile confrontations, and her methods of dialogue and empathy sometimes just didn’t work. When she did draw her weapon, she had to be sure that the shot would not kill. Those few times she failed she would scratch another line into both her implement and her arm. The list would serve as a reminder of how many drinks she would need to order whenever she started to hear their bloodied gurgles and pained screams again. He had four belts lined with pistols, two rifles on his shoulders and a whole arsenal of bombs. He was not scared of any kind of company or gang - he had enough gunpowder for them all. He left scorched earth and graveyards of slavers everywhere he passed. As it tends to go, he would meet his end in one of his massive shootouts, peppered by bullets and finally stabbed and beaten to death by some armed adolescents. In his last few breaths he thought back to where he made the mistake, reliving his final standoff. It was not there. He thought further back, searched far and wide for the cause of his death. He didn’t have time to finish his thesis, but in this oblivious state it occurred to him that no matter how many of them he killed, more would rise up to take their place. With hundreds of souls to his name and fear in his heart, he considered if he did the right thing. Gunlingers are outlaws and vigilantes, those who wander the world in search of a purpose with a firearm at their side. They see their guns as the great equalizers that they are and use them to attempt to change the world through “civilized” violence. Whether they do this by a single, devastating headshot or a barrage of fire, you can be sure to expect them not to miss their mark. Most gunslingers die as they lived – alone, full of energy and pondering all of the things they still didn’t change.

## Class Features
#### Showdown — *Passive*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 0*
**Effect:** You gain 1 Empowered Shot Charge whenever you perform a ranged attack. Upon reaching the maximum of 3 Charges, you may spend them while performing any pistol ranged attack in order to empower it: Zenith: This attack gains Pierce 3 and +3 DMG. Nadir: This attack heals 4 SN and gains +2 ACC.

#### < Quickdraw — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 1 · Weapons: Pistol*
**2 SN** · +1 ACC · +3 DMG  
**Effect:** Triggers upon entering combat. This attack is only usable against targets who have not yet acted.

#### | Lightning Hands — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 2 · Weapons: Pistol*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** Gains +2 DMG when used against a target who hasn’t had a turn during this combat round.

## Path: Marauder
*Path that fully locks into rapid and powerful barrages.*

#### Morning Sun — *Passive*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 2*
**Effect:** Holstering Pistol and Rifles is a free action. Your Pistol and Rifles gain +1 DMG per attack you perform with either of them in a turn until the end of that turn. Showdown gains the following option at 3 Charges: Mayhem: Gain 3 AP and suffer 4 Strain. You may use abilities again this turn.

#### >>> Faceoff — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 3 · Weapons: Pistol*
**6 Strain** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** You may target any number of additional targets with this attack and it costs 2 additional Strain per target beyond the first.

#### >> Wasteful Barrage — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 4*
**3 Strain** · +3 EVA  
**Effect:** Empty your weapon’s entire magazine into a target in cover. The target and other creatures within its A suffer 1 DMG per ammo you depleted this way.

## Path: Drifter
*Path that shifts more into stealth and evasion, increasing the class’ frontline survivability.*

#### Evening Breeze — *Passive*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 2*
**Effect:** While you are in Stealth, restore 2 VG while at less than half of your max VG and reduce the level of all damaging status effects by 1 at the end of your turn. Showdown gains the following option at 3 Charges: Fade: Enter Stealth if you end your turn in cover and out of direct line of sight of your enemies.

#### >> Masterful Shot — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 3*
**6 Strain** · +2 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** You may activate any Empowered Shot effect without spending charges when you perform this ability. Gain +2 EVA until your next turn.

#### < Pain Tolerance — *Utility*
*Requires: Gunslinger Rank 4*
**5 Strain**  
**Effect:** Triggers on you being damaged by an enemy. Gain a temporary destiny point that lasts until the end of your next turn.
