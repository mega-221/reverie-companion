# Mysticism

<!-- chapter: 06-disciplines | pages: 169-173 -->  
**Ponder your ideals, reshape the world.**  
*Recommended for cryptic mages, wise shamans and all those with an interest for the occult aspects of Seasons.*  

## Abilities

#### >> Parting Commune — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Reanimate a creature that died in the last 5 minutes. It will persist in a delirious state for 1d6 minutes during which it can answer simple questions. It cannot move on its own. After the ability’s duration the creature permanently dies and cannot be reanimated again.

#### >> Raise Zombie — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**5 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Reanimate and control a creature whose body is somewhat intact. It can perform basic abilities, its EVA is halved and rounded down and it deals -1 DMG. The creature will persist for a number of rounds equal to its max VG/2, rounded down or until you use this ability again.

#### < Puppeteer — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Triggers on an enemy with more than half of their SL performing an ability. You may change the targets of that ability if it has them, and you may spend the target’s movement action or make them use a free movement action before doing so.

#### >>> Revenant — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**15 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Reanimate and control a fresh creature with a somewhat intact body. It can perform any abilities it once knew, its EVA, DMG and all other rolls are reduced by 1 for each point of VG it had under 0 when it died. The creature persists indefinitely or until the caster creates a new revenant.

#### < Final Safeguard — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**8 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Halice Triggers on you or any other creature suffering damage that would kill it or otherwise put its VG below 0. Use your mastery of the flesh to anchor the soul of the target to their body for a bit longer, healing them back to 0 VG and bypassing any death’s door checks. This ability may be performed without using up a reaction if its user suffers +5 SN.

#### > Vital Burst — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Add +2 ACC to your next attack or gain 2 TVG.

#### >> Cauterize — *Defensive*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**4 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Cleanse all Bleeding and restore 1 VG to a target within your C. For targets below half of their maximum VG or yourself, also restore VG equal to your class rank plus the rank of Bleeding this ability cleansed.

#### >> Blazing Armaments — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Your weapons inflict Burn 2 for the next 3 attacks you perform.

#### | Firefly — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor You gain +2 Tiles of movement until your next turn and inflict Burn 1 on those you pass by in A during this period.

#### | Living Torch — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Vamor Reduce your VG to 1. Your next attack gains +10 ACC and inflicts Burn 5 and you are immune to DMG until you perform it or until the end of your next turn, whichever happens sooner. If used outside of combat, you may immediately perform any roll with a +6 bonus but you are reduced to 0 VG after the roll. Requires VG above 1.

#### > Telepathy — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**1 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Convey a short message to a creature within 1km.

#### >> Fate Sliver — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Roll 1d6 and note down the result. You may exchange that die with any other die of a roll you witness during that day. Usable once per day.

#### >> Bound Assistance — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma This ability additionally costs 1 Destiny Point if it has already been used this day. Summon a soul from the void to perform one of the following: Rupture: Explode in a 2x2 tile area you can see, dealing 3 DMG and inflicting Burn 2. Obscure: Give a creature you can see +4 EVA until your next turn. Bestow: You gain a temporary Destiny Point that lasts for a day that cannot be used to upgrade Convictions.

#### < Deep Attunement — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**6 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Triggers on you or an ally you see using a Destiny Point to gain a Conviction bonus on a roll. Double the Conviction bonus on that roll.

#### >> Martyrdom — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**8 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Bruma Reduce the Failing of a target by any number of ranks by spending 2 Destiny Points per rank of the Failing. You may reduce the ranks without paying the DP cost by increasing one of your Failings by that many ranks.

#### >> Remote Observation — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 0*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Take control of a nearby Observer in a high place and survey the region surrounding you. You gain +2 to all rolls concerning navigation while within that area.

#### >>> Surveillance — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 1*
**3 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Take control of a nearby Observer for 1 minute and survey a nearby location. You gain +2 to all rolls concerning navigation and stealth while within that area.

#### >> Watcher’s Summons — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst This ability additionally costs 1 Destiny Point if it has already been used this day. Summon a nearby Observer to perform one of the following: Blind: The Observer attacks its target’s eyes, making them suffer -4 ACC during their next 2 turns. Distract: The Observer distracts the target who performs a CL 16 Willpower check and becomes Unaware until their next turn if they don’t succeed. Wound: The Observer uses its metal claws in an attempt to wound the target, inflicting Bleeding equal to your highest Trumst Conviction rank.

#### >> /> Suffocating Omen — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 3*
**4/3 SN** · +0 EVA  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Several nearby Observers arrange themselves in a cross pattern above a 4x4 tile area. For as long as this ability remains channeled, the creatures within its area have their movement speed capped at 1 tile and suffer Feeble 3.

#### >> Transcendent Messenger — *Utility*
*Requires: Class Rank 4*
**20 SN**  
**Effect:** Conviction: Trumst Take control of an Observer in any area you are familiar with for 5 minutes. You may speak through the Observer.
