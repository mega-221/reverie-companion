# What Constitutes a Character

<!-- chapter: 04-characters-in-depth | pages: 34-34 -->

Each player character and important non player character has the following
mechanical traits and stats which make them unique:
● Attributes
● Core and special skills
● Vigor, strain and evasion
● Convictions and failings
● Origin
● Tier and levels
● Class
● Disciplines
● Aspects
Out of these, all of the concepts that have not yet been explained will be introduced
in this chapter and expanded to list all of their options in the following chapters.
