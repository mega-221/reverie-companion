# Mechanist

<!-- chapter: 05-classes | pages: 110-113 -->  
**Archetype:** Scholar · Constructs · RemoteControl  
**Recommended disciplines:** Echo  

> It finally moved! She had been working on the little automaton for months, and she finally managed to get it to move. She was starting to get frustrated, not knowing whether it was the design of the motor and the legs or her ambedic link to the little machine that was at fault. For someone who was proud to exclaim that she would never mother a child, she sure seemed excited to witness her creation shift and shake, even if it ultimately was just a cold construct. He sat behind tall cover, a blindfold over his eyes. He used the first construct to plant a breach charge on the enemy’s wall. Once it blew open, the second construct flew into the building, equipped with a flamethrower and an oil spewing mechanism. Luckily, he had recently installed a transmission dampener onto his ambedic receiver in order to block out unnecessary emotions and increase his efficiency at piloting such a horrifying machine. After a few rounds of drinks, he was even able to forget the face of the child that was inside the building. She stood inside her construct-suit, placed in between the firing line and the wounded civilians behind her. Before charging into the gunmen she recalled how sad she felt when she disassembled her old construct so she could use its battery to power this monstrous suit. As the bullets rained upon her firm but still destructible shell, she could swear she heard it thank her. Mechanists are pioneers of robotics, assembling and piloting the very first of such creations. They combine their knowledge of mechanics and ambedic technology in order to create remotely-controllable extensions of their body in the form of robotic companions. Due to the great mental load operating such a construct demands, the very best of the mechanists can only ever optimally operate two smaller constructs or a single more complex one at any given moment.

## Class Features
#### Sensory Meld — *Passive*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 0*
**Effect:** You may suffer VG damage instead of Strain for performing Echo, Construct and Mechanist Class abilities.

#### | Overclock — *Utility*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 1*
**2 SN**  
**Effect:** A construct you are paired with gains +2 ACC, +1 DMG dealt and +1 Tile of movement until your next turn. Make a CL 14 Engineering check. On a failure, the construct suffers a Glitch.

#### < Breaking Point — *Utility*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 2*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers upon construct having 0 or less HP or otherwise shutting down. Prevent the construct from shutting down until your next turn.

## Path: Operator
*Path that focuses on multitasking and control of multiple constructs.*

#### Mind Fragmentation — *Passive*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 2*
**Effect:** You can be paired with two Constructs at the same time and you can perform two command abilities or perform the same command ability twice in a single turn provided they target two different Constructs. At the beginning of your turn you may lose your Movement action to gain 1 AP that can only be spent on command abilities targeting constructs.

#### >> Target Triangulation — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 3*
**4 SN** · +0 ACC · +0 DMG  
**Effect:** This attack gains +3 ACC for each of your paired constructs within C of the target. After performing this ability, your allies gain the same ACC bonuses against that target until your next turn or until the constructs are no longer in the correct range.

#### < Bodily Extension — *Utility*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 4*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on an enemy attack hitting you or an ally. If it was a melee attack, any construct you are paired with within your or your ally’s M may come to the target’s aid to a tile adjacent to them and redirect the attack to itself. If it was a ranged attack, any construct you are paired with within M of that attack’s trajectory may move into it and redirect the attack to itself.

## Path: Pilot
*Path that directly interfaces with its large construct mech, providing precise control over its actions.*

#### Load Dampening — *Passive*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 2*
**Effect:** The cost of regaining AP while piloting a mech costs you only 2 Strain per AP. You may regain the 4th AP in a turn that costs 4 Strain and requires a CL 18 Engineering check. On a failure, the Mech suffers a Glitch.

#### < Subsystem Optimization — *Utility*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 3*
**4 SN**  
**Effect:** Triggers on the start of your turn while you are piloting a Mech. You may eliminate the cooldown of your Mech’s subsystem. Make a CL 18 Engineering check. On a failure, the Mech suffers a Glitch.

#### >>> Ambedic Pulse — *Ranged Attack*
*Requires: Mechanist Rank 4*
**10 SN** · 0 ACC · 2 DMG  
**Effect:** Disturb your mech’s power source, inflicting SN equal to your own SN to all creatures within M of the Mech. This attack cannot miss and cannot deal overflowing DMG. You are ejected from the Mech.
